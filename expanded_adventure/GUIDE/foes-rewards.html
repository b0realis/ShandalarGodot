Foes and rewards

Every duel on your island is against a foe of foes.json: a fixed deck, a fixed life total and a face. The island’s roaming creatures can take your decks too. A won quest can pay in cards from rewards.json.

Foes

foes maps an id of your own to a foe. Quests name their foe by that id, and so do the finale and the roaming decks.

name
The opponent’s name in the duel and the panes, at most 60 characters.
deck
Card name to count (1 to 40 each), 1 to 250 cards in all. The order is kept.
life
1 to 99. The foe’s life is never scaled to the hero.
face
A creature row of the game’s creature book (1 to 200; not row 0, the lair): the foe wears that creature’s face. The Refuge’s are 11, 18, 4, 32, 25 and 6.
deck_name
Optional: the foe’s deck name in the duel; by default the quest’s title (the finale’s: the region’s title).

Card names

Every card a plugin names, in a deck or a reward bundle, must be a card of a card pack the manifest requires (from the pack’s trusted list, shipped with the game, never from your zip) or a card of the base game, written exactly as the game names it. The validator checks the names when it runs in the game folder; the game checks them again when it reads the plugin.

How a plugin duel is played

  • The hero plays their current deck with their own life, padded as a creature meeting pads a short deck.
  • No ante is staked. A loss or a draw changes nothing: the hero can prepare and try again.
  • The computer plays the foe at the campaign’s difficulty, with the 1997 mulligan rule and a random choice of who plays first.
  • A duel cannot begin while the reason in deck.no_pack, deck.empty or deck.unknown applies: the plugin’s card packs are not enabled, the deck is empty, or it holds a card the game does not know. A pack that is enabled but older than the plugin’s min_version is said in the game’s own words: “The plugin name needs pack version or newer (installed version).”

Roaming creatures

With a roaming section, the creatures walking the island can fight with your decks instead of their own:

decks
Up to 10 entries. A creature takes the first entry whose colors hold its own color (one of
  • black
  • blue
  • green
  • red
  • white
); it keeps its name and its gifts, and takes the foe’s deck and life (or the entry’s life). name is the deck’s name in the duel, and the creature says “My spells are those of the name.” when it meets the hero.
min_tier
Roaming creatures on the island are at least this tier (0 to 30). Lairs are untouched.
skip
Which creatures keep their own deck: those for which (world seed × seed + slot × slot + creature row × row) is a multiple of every. It is fixed by the world, never by the hero’s strength or hidden cards. every: 0 skips none.
ante
random_nonbasic: the creature stakes one random non-basic card of its new deck. creature: it stakes what it would have staked.

Only ordinary roaming meetings take a plugin deck: never a quest’s creature, a town’s, a siege, or a creature that summons or swaps before its duel.

Rewards

The effect {"reward_choice": true} in a quest’s complete (once at most) owes the hero one choice. At a pane with the rewards action (and in every quest pane), Choose reward cards opens the bundles; each offers count cards and the hero takes one bundle per choice owed.

  • A bundle offers its cards in order, going round its list again until it has count cards (1 to 15, default 6), and leaves out what the campaign’s copy limits and ban list forbid; cards already waiting count as owned. A bundle that cannot fill count cannot be chosen yet, and the choice stays owed.
  • The chosen cards wait in the island’s progress and go into the bag while it has room; Collect waiting cards, or the effect claim_cards, moves more of them later.
  • label is a text key: the bundle’s button and the {color} of rewards.offer.
  • rewards.json is optional; without it no quest may owe a reward choice.
