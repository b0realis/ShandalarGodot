Places: sites plugins

A sites plugin adds places, not a land: a hut in the woods, a wizards’ tower, a shrine by the road. Each place is a picture, a sprite on the map and panes of buttons. It stands on Shandalar’s mainland, on the islands a campaign sails to, or on both.

What a sites plugin is

A sites plugin is a plugin of its own kind, new in plugin API 2. It has no island, no ship, no port, no quests and no row in the Tasks list. It has places. A player ticks it at the Embark like any plugin, and its places stand in every adventure it is ticked for.

A place offers services. A service has a price in gold, and it can raise a dungeon, set a flag, give gold or food, write in the journal, or copy one of the hero’s cards. A price can climb with each use, and a service can end when its last price is paid.

The safety rule is the same as an island’s: data only, never code. A place is a list of panes, a condition is one of a closed list, an effect is one of a closed list, and the game’s own interpreter reads them. A sites plugin may need card packs, as an island may (requires), and every card it names must be a card of the base game or of a pack it needs.

The game ships two, each a finished place and a study to read: An Old Adventurer’s Hut and the Conjuration Wizard Guild. They are walked through below.

The manifest

plugin.json is an island’s manifest (Your first plugin) with three differences:

FieldRule
kind"sites". Without it a plugin is an island ("island" is the default).
api_version2 or newer; a sites plugin declaring API 1 is refused. A game older than API 2 refuses every sites plugin: it names kind as a field it does not know.
regionNone. A sites plugin has no island to name, and a region refuses it.

Everything else is as for an island: id, name, version, authors, description, license, learning_note, requires, minimum_game_version, and the files list that export writes. The id begins every picture key and every button the game makes for the plugin, and saves keep the plugin’s progress under it: never change it. The Hut’s manifest:

A wrong manifest is named in one of these sentences:

  • plugin.json: kind must be one of island, sites.
  • plugin.json: a sites plugin needs api_version 2 or newer.
  • plugin.json: a sites plugin has no region; its places are described in sites.json.

Its files

A sites plugin’s content is two files:

  • sites.json
  • text.json

sites.json holds its places, their pictures and sprites, panes and services, its flags and its dungeons. text.json is the same file as an island’s: every sentence, title and button by key (Text and placeholders). Beside them go the pictures, a README.txt and a LICENSE.txt. An island’s files (world.json and the rest) are not read in a sites plugin. The package rules, the limits and the files list are an island’s (Testing).

sites.json, field by field

FieldRule
saltA whole number from 0 to 2147483647, default 0. Mixed into the plugin’s own dice, which place its places and its dungeons. Give each plugin its own, so two plugins never draw the same tiles.
flagsThe plugin’s own switches, at most 32, all off at the start of a campaign (Flags).
picturesPicture keys to files of the plugin, at most 32. Every key begins with the plugin’s id and _, as an island’s do (Art and music).
dungeonsThe dungeons its places can raise, by key, at most 8 (Dungeons).
sitesRequired: its places, by key, 1 to 8. A key is 1 to 32 lower-case letters, digits or _, a letter first. Saves know a place by its key: never change it.

A place

FieldRule
nameRequired, at most 60 characters, shown as written. The place’s name on the map, and its pane’s title unless the pane gives one.
whereWhich lands it stands on: mainland, islands or both. Default ["mainland"] (Where places stand).
pictureRequired: the pane’s picture, a picture key of sites.json or a skin picture.
spriteRequired: how it stands on the map, {"file": "art/…png", "width": 104}, its width 16 to 512 world pixels (a tile is 128 wide). It stands on its tile as a marker’s sprite does: the picture’s transparent margins trimmed, the middle of its bottom edge on the tile (Map sprites).
gap1 to 8 tiles, default 3: how far it keeps from towns, castles, dungeons and other places.
panesRequired, 1 to 8 (Panes).

Where places stand

A place’s where takes either or both of:

  • mainland
  • islands

mainland puts one on Shandalar’s mainland. islands puts one on the island of every island plugin the campaign sails to. A place with both is one place in many spots: its uses are counted once for all of them.

  1. When. On the mainland, when the campaign begins; a place new to an adventure (a new place in an update of the plugin) the next time the adventure is loaded. On an island, the first time the hero lands there.
  2. Which tile. A land tile of the land the hero starts on (on an island, the port’s land): no town, road or dungeon on it, and at least gap tiles from every town, village, castle, dungeon, anchorage, harbor and other place. Next to a road when such a tile exists, so the hero walks past it.
  3. The dice. The tile is drawn on the plugin’s own dice, seeded from the campaign’s seed, the plugin’s salt and the place’s key. The same campaign seed puts it on the same tile every time. The adventure’s own dice are never touched: with a sites plugin ticked, the towns, dungeons, creatures and every draw of the game are the same as without it.
  4. Kept. Once placed, the tile is saved with the land and the place never moves. When no tile fits, the place does not stand on that land. A land holds at most 64 places.

On the map the place is drawn by its sprite and listed on the map and the minimap. Walking onto it opens its pane, which pushes the wandering creatures back as a town’s does. Its places stand while the plugin is ticked for the adventure, installed and valid, and every card pack it needs is one of the campaign’s, switched on and as new as the plugin asks.

Panes and their when

A place shows the first of its panes whose when holds. A when is a list of conditions that must all hold, and an empty one always holds. So list the special panes first and end with one that has no when. The Hut chooses its pane by its dungeon’s state: cleared, open, or not yet raised.

FieldRule
whenConditions, at most 16. Default [], which always holds.
titleA text key. Default: the place’s name.
subtitleA text key. Default: the plugin’s name from its manifest.
pictureA picture key. Default: the place’s picture.
linesAt most 16. Each a text key, or {"text": "key", "when": [...]}: a line said only while its conditions hold.
actionsThe pane’s buttons, at most 8 (Actions).

Actions

An action is a button. Its do is one of:

  • effects
  • copy_card
  • leave
doWhat a press does
effectsPays the price, counts the use, then runs the action’s effects in order. Then the pane shows its result lines with a Return and a Leave button; without result, the place’s pane comes back at once.
copy_cardLists the hero’s cards under pick_title and pick_line. The card picked is copied into the bag, the price paid and the use counted; then the result lines (default site.copied), and the journal says site.copied_log. Only a card the hero holds can be copied, and under the Classic MTG rules not one they already hold every copy of that the rules allow: the wise man’s rule for his copy. The copy needs room in the bag.
leaveCloses the pane. It costs nothing and is never counted.
FieldRule
idRequired: 1 to 32 lower-case letters, digits or _, a letter first. The action’s name in its place. Its uses are counted by it, and conditions name it.
doRequired: effects, copy_card or leave.
labelThe button’s text key. Default site.pay (“Pay {price} gold”), and site.leave for a leave.
whenConditions; the button is offered only while they hold. Default [].
pricesAt most 16 prices in gold, each 0 to 1,000,000. Default []: free, and never used up (Prices).
effectsAt most 16 effects. Only an action that does effects has them.
resultLines shown once the action is done, written as a pane’s lines. Default [].
pick_title, pick_linecopy_card only: the card list’s title and the line under it. Defaults site.pick_title and site.pick_line.

A button the hero cannot use now is shown grayed: they lack its price, or, for a copy, hold no card that may be copied or have no room in the bag. Should a press still fail, nothing is paid and the pane says why (site.no_gold, site.bag_full, site.no_cards, site.no_room).

One id, one action

The same action may stand in several panes, written the same in each, field for field. Two different actions with one id refuse the plugin, and so do two leave buttons with one id that differ in any field, a label say: sites.json: sites.shrine.panes[1].actions[1].id: leave names another action of this site; an id is one action (the same action may stand in several panes). Copy a button word for word into every pane, or give it another id.

Prices, and how an action is used up

The first use of an action costs its first price, the second use its second price, and so on. Once every price is paid, the action is used up: its button is gone from every pane, and the condition used_up holds for it. An action with no prices is free and never used up. A price of 0 is a free use that still counts.

Uses are counted for the plugin as a whole. The Guild’s tower stands on the mainland and on every island, and all its towers share one count: four copies in all, wherever they are made. The Guild’s copy action:

Its label, Copy a card ({price} gold, {uses_left} left), reads “Copy a card (2000 gold, 4 left)” the first time and “(12000 gold, 1 left)” the last.

Conditions

A condition is an object with exactly one of these keys. In a list, every one must hold; an empty list always holds. At most 16 in a list. These are a sites plugin’s own: an island’s conditions (stage, quest_done and the rest, Quests and steps) are not read here, and these are not read in an island.

  • flag
  • not_flag
  • gold_at_least
  • on_mainland
  • used
  • used_up
  • dungeon
ConditionExample valueHolds when
flag"told"The flag is set. It must be one of sites.json’s flags.
not_flag"told"The flag is not set.
gold_at_least1000The hero carries at least that much gold (0 to 1,000,000).
on_mainlandtrueThe place stands on the mainland (true) or on an island (false).
used{"action": "copy", "min": 1}This place’s action was done at least min and at most max times (defaults 0 and 1000).
used_up"copy"This place’s action has used all its prices. Only an action with prices can be used up; naming one without is refused.
dungeon{"dungeon": "vault", "state": "open"}The plugin’s dungeon of that key, on the land the hero stands on, is in that state: none (not raised here), open (it still holds cards) or cleared.

The states of dungeon:

  • none
  • open
  • cleared

Effects

An effect is an object with exactly one of these keys. An action’s effects run in order, after its price is paid. At most 16 in a list. Like the conditions, these are a sites plugin’s own.

  • set_flag
  • clear_flag
  • gold
  • food
  • log_clue
  • log_event
  • spawn_dungeon
EffectExample valueWhat it does
set_flag"told"Sets the flag.
clear_flag"told"Clears it.
gold50Gold to the hero, from −100,000 to 100,000; it never falls below 0.
food6Food, from −1,000 to 1,000; never below 0.
log_clue{"text": "key"}A clue in the journal.
log_event{"event": "hut", "text": "key", "done": false}A line in the journal; event is a word of your own.
spawn_dungeon"vault"Raises the dungeon of that key on this land, unless one of it is open here already (Dungeons). When no dungeon can be raised here, the whole action is refused and nothing is paid.

Flags

A flag is a switch of your own, declared in sites.json’s flags (at most 32). Every flag is off at the start of a campaign. Effects set and clear it, and conditions read it. A plugin’s flags are one set for the whole adventure, the same on every land. A flag a condition or effect names that flags does not declare refuses the plugin.

Dungeons

A dungeon of sites.json is a recipe that spawn_dungeon raises. It belongs to no quest. Its treasure is the cards the plugin lists.

FieldRule
nameRequired, at most 60 characters. Its name on the map, in its pane and in {dungeon}.
colorIts creatures’ color, default random (drawn on the plugin’s dice when it is raised).
sizeIts creatures’ size, default small.
rulesUp to 5 of the 1997 dungeon rules: no_color (no cards of its color), no_artifacts, no_instants (no instants or interrupts), life_carries (life lost carries over), life_adds (life left is added to the next duel). Default none.
cardsRequired: its treasure, 1 to 6 cards of the base game or of a card pack the plugin requires.
distanceHow far from the place it lies, in tiles: min 2 to 40 (default 6), max 4 to 64 (default 24). min may not be more than max.

The colors, sizes and rules:

  • black
  • blue
  • green
  • red
  • white
  • random
  • small
  • large
  • no_color
  • no_artifacts
  • no_instants
  • life_carries
  • life_adds

How one is raised. It rises on the land the hero stands on, on a land tile of the place’s own land where a dungeon may stand, at least min and at most max tiles from the hero. When no tile is within that distance, it takes one of the nearest tiles past min. The tile is drawn on the plugin’s dice. Its color, size, rules, cards and name are the recipe’s, and everything about it is known: its place on the map, its creatures and its rules, as if the hero had heard every clue.

Then it is an ordinary dungeon. The hero enters it, fights its creatures and takes its cards as in any dungeon. It is drawn on the map and listed with the others, and it moves when left, as 1997’s dungeons do. It belongs to the land, not to the plugin: if the plugin goes missing, the dungeon stays.

  • One dungeon of a key is open on a land at a time. Once it is cleared, a new spawn_dungeon of that key raises a new one, on a new draw. To sell a dungeon once, give its action one price, as the Hut does.
  • A land holds at most 48 castles and dungeons in all, the game’s own counted. When the land is full, or no tile fits, the action is refused with site.no_room and nothing is paid.
  • Put spawn_dungeon before a log_clue or log_event whose words name {dungeon}: the name is known only once the dungeon is raised.

Words and placeholders

text.json works as an island’s (Text and placeholders). API 2 adds five placeholders for places:

PlaceholderIs replaced by
{price}An action’s price this time; after a press, the price just paid.
{uses_left}How many more times the action can be done; empty for an action without prices.
{gold}The gold the hero carries.
{card}The card just copied.
{dungeon}The name of the dungeon this plugin raised on this land (with several, the last of sites.json’s dungeons that stands here).

A place also fills {hero}, the hero’s name, and {region}, the land’s title: “Shandalar” on the mainland, the island’s title on an island. Each place fills its own set; a placeholder not filled there is shown as written:

Progress and saves

Save version 9 keeps a sites plugin’s progress, all of it under the plugin’s id:

  • hero.adventures: the plugin’s row, as an island’s, with "kind": "sites".
  • sites.id: uses, each action’s count by "place.action"; flags, the names set; dungeons, each dungeon raised by "land:dungeon".
  • Each land’s map keeps its places’ tiles (expansion.sites, by "plugin:place"), and a raised dungeon is part of its land like any dungeon.

A save from before version 9 opens with no sites progress. A plugin missing in the middle of a campaign: the save still opens, its places hide, and their tiles and the plugin’s progress are kept, so putting it back brings everything back. A dungeon it raised stays an ordinary dungeon. A sites plugin never has a ship, a note on the mainland or a Tasks row, so nothing else changes. A card pack it needs switched off hides its places the same way.

Updates. Saves know a sites plugin by its id, a place by its key, an action by its id and a raised dungeon by its key. Change one of them and what was saved under it is lost: a renamed action starts again from its first price. Everything else is safe: words, pictures, panes, conditions and effects are read fresh; a new place is placed on the next load; prices may change (a save’s uses so far are kept, so do not shorten a price list or give a free action prices: a save that used it more often finds it used up); a new salt, gap or where reaches only lands where the place is not placed yet. Compare every release with the one players have:

$ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py compat dist/old/my_places.zip dist/my_places.zip

For a sites plugin compat lists a place removed or renamed, an action id removed and a dungeon key removed, and exits 1. It also warns, and exits 1, when a save may find an action used up: a free action that now has prices (its free uses were counted too) and a price list made shorter. It notes, and exits 0 for, a place added, a price list made longer or given other prices, an action made free, a dungeon recipe changed, a flag removed, and a new where, gap or salt.

The two examples, walked through

Both are in the game folder’s expanded_adventure/, need no card pack, and are original fiction, not official Magic canon.

An Old Adventurer’s Hut

A hut in the mainland’s woods. The very old man inside was a planeswalker once, from another world. For 1000 gold, once, he tells the hero where a dungeon lies that nobody else knows of. The plugin holds one place, one dungeon, three panes and one service. Its dungeon first:

The Forgotten Vault is large, its color drawn when it rises, 8 to 22 tiles from the hut, and its treasure is three cards. The place stands on the mainland alone:

Its three panes are chosen by the vault’s state on this land, the special ones first. While the vault is cleared, the pane says: "So you found my old treasures," the old man smiles. "Keep them well. I have nothing left to sell, only peace, and that I keep for myself." While it is open: The old man looks up from the fire. "You know the way to {dungeon}. Go, before its guardians grow bolder." Before it is raised, the last pane, with no when, tells his story and makes his offer:

One price, so the secret is sold once. The press pays 1000 gold, raises the vault, writes a clue and a journal line, and shows the two result lines. spawn_dungeon comes first, so the clue can name the vault: The old man in the woods told you where {dungeon} lies. The button’s label is Pay {price} gold for the secret. Every pane’s leave is written the same, as one action.

Conjuration Wizard Guild

A wizards’ tower whose conjurers copy a card the hero carries, for 2000, 4000, 8000 and 12000 gold, and then never again. Its tower stands on the mainland and on every island:

Two panes. The first holds once the copy action is used up: "We have copied four cards for you," the guild master says, "and the guild's law allows no more. Go well." The second offers the copy (quoted above), and a line that greets a returning hero only after the first copy:

The copy’s result line names the card and the price paid: The conjurers chant over your {card} until a second one lies beside it, warm as fresh ink. A copy of {card} is yours, for {price} gold.

Tools for places

The authoring tool builds a place with you, step by step. Each step is one command of tools/adventure_plugin.py, run in the game folder; on Windows type python and \ as on the first page. The loop:

  • new --kind sites
  • add-place
  • add-dungeon
  • walk
  • art
  • validate
  • the Lab
  • export

  1. Start a sites plugin

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py new my_places --id my_places --name "My Places" --kind sites

    It writes a sites plugin that loads and plays: one place, a wayside shrine on the mainland and on every island, whose service costs 20, 40 and 80 gold and is then used up, with a small stand-in picture and map sprite.

  2. Add a place

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-place my_places --id tower --name "Glass Tower" --where mainland,islands --service copy --prices 300,600

    add-place writes the place into sites.json: a pane with an intro line, the service and a Leave button, and for a priced service the pane shown once it is used up. Its words go into text.json as tower.*, ready to rewrite, and a stand-in pane picture and map sprite into art/, their picture key registered. --service is effects (the default: pay, then effects), copy (copy a card) or none; --where is mainland (the default), islands or both; --prices are the prices in turn. An id the plugin already has, or an island plugin, is refused in one line. The two JSON files are written again whole, tab-indented.

  3. Add a dungeon

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-dungeon my_places --id cellar --name "The Dry Cellar" --cards "Lightning Bolt,Llanowar Elves" --size large --place tower --price 250

    add-dungeon adds the recipe: --cards (1 to 6, joined by commas), --color, --size, --rules, --min and --max. With --place it also gives that place a button that raises the dungeon for --price gold (default 1000), and the Hut’s three panes: two new panes before the place’s others, for the dungeon cleared and open, with their words. --action names the button; name an effects action the place already has and spawn_dungeon is put first in its effects instead. The card names are checked as validate checks them, and an edit that would refuse the plugin changes nothing.

  4. Walk it

    walk plays each place as text, without the game. It shows the pane with no gold, then presses every ready service in turn, clears each dungeon it raised, and shows the pane after each step: the words filled in, each button pressable (x) or not, and why not. The Hut:

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py walk expanded_adventure/Old_Adventurers_Hut

       "But I still know things nobody else in Shandalar knows. For 1000 gold I will tell you where a dungeon lies that no wise man has ever heard of."
       ( ) Pay 1000 gold for the secret   not ready: You need 1000 gold for this; you carry 0.
  ...
       (x) Pay 1000 gold for the secret
       (x) Leave

     dungeon raised: forgotten_vault (open)

       The way to The Forgotten Vault is marked on your map.
       (x) Return  (x) Leave

    The walk follows the game’s own rules for panes, conditions, prices and effects. A place that stands on the mainland and on the islands is walked once for each. It has no world: a dungeon always finds room, and the hero always holds one card to copy. --site KEY walks one place, --gold N sets the hero’s gold (default 100000; try a small sum to see what the hero cannot afford), --json prints the steps for your own scripts.

  5. Look at the art

    art measures the pictures and gives advice. It never refuses a plugin. The Guild:

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py art expanded_adventure/Conjuration_Wizard_Guild
Conjuration Wizard Guild (tower)
  pane picture  art/conjuration_guild_tower.png: 1024 x 560, 1.83 : 1 (the pane's frame is 512 x 280, 1.83 : 1)
  map sprite    art/conjuration_guild_map.png: 320 x 400, drawn 92 world pixels wide (a tile is 128 x 64)
    trimmed to 276 x 384 at 22, 13 (48% of the picture transparent); drawn 92 x 128; its foot in the middle
    3 source pixels to a world pixel: a whole number, so pixel art stays crisp.
0 pieces of advice. Advice only: art never refuses a plugin (validate does that).
    • The pane picture. The pane’s frame is 512 × 280, about 1.83 to 1, and the picture is cropped to fill it. Advice comes for another shape, and for a picture under 512 pixels wide.
    • The map sprite. Its transparent margins are trimmed, and the rest is drawn width world pixels wide; a tile is 128 × 64. Advice comes for a sprite with no transparency (its background would be drawn as a block), for a foot that is not in the middle of the bottom edge (the point that stands on the tile), and for a width over 128.
    • The scale. The source pixels to one world pixel. A whole number, as the Hut’s and the Guild’s 3, keeps pixel art crisp; otherwise art names the widths that would make it whole.

    A PNG’s transparency is read; for a JPEG, a WebP or an unusual PNG art says what it did not check. It reads an island plugin’s port and castle the same way.

  6. Validate, the Lab, export

    validate, info, export and compat work on a sites plugin as on an island (Testing). info lists its places, where they stand, their actions and its dungeons. A sites problem names the place in sites.json, for example sites.json: sites.shrine.panes[1].actions[0].effects[2].spawn_dungeon: no dungeon vault in sites.json dungeons.

    In a source checkout, ./tools/expansion_lab.sh --out lab-my_places --plugin my_places then plays the plugin in the game itself, headless, for each seed (Testing): it places the places on a fresh mainland and on a small test island, opens each pane and presses every button it offers with gold enough, checks every dungeon raised, saves and reads the adventure back, and takes the plugin away to see its places hide and its progress kept.

    Last, in the game: put the folder in the plugins folder, start a new adventure, tick the plugin in Expanded adventure plugins... and embark. A place on the mainland stands near a road; open the map to find it. For a place on the islands, tick an island plugin too and sail there. After a change to words, panes or prices, Rescan or start the game again and load your save; a place is placed once, so a new salt, gap or where shows in a new adventure. Then export the zip to share.
