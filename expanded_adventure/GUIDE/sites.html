Places: sites plugins

A sites plugin adds places, not a land: a hut in the woods, a wizards’ tower, a shrine by the road. Each place is a picture, a sprite on the map and panes of buttons. It stands on Shandalar’s mainland, on the islands a campaign sails to, or on both.

Start here: the wizard

Never made a plugin, or used a command line? Open a terminal in the game’s folder and type one line:

$ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py wizard

It asks a handful of questions, each with numbered answers and a default on Enter: what your plugin is called, what the place is called, which of the twelve recipes it is, that recipe’s few numbers and names (a price, a card, an opponent and one of five stock decks), and where it stands. Then it writes the plugin, checks it as the game will, plays it once as text, and prints the commands that come next. Nothing is written before the last question is answered; q leaves. Run it again with the plugin’s folder to add another place: wizard My_Places. Then look at what you made:

$ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py preview My_Places

That writes one page, preview/my_places.html, with every pane as the game lays it out (Preview it). The rest of this page is for when you want to know why it works.

What a sites plugin is

A sites plugin is a plugin of its own kind, new in plugin API 2. It has no island, no ship, no port and no quests of an island’s kind. It has places, and from plugin API 3 on its places may talk, take costs, roll, duel, trade cards and put rows of their own into the Tasks list (Plugin API 3). A player ticks it at the Embark like any plugin, and its places stand in every adventure it is ticked for.

A place offers services. A service has a price in gold, and it can raise a dungeon, set a flag, give gold or food, write in the journal, or copy one of the hero’s cards. A price can climb with each use, and a service can end when its last price is paid.

The safety rule is the same as an island’s: data only, never code. A place is a list of panes, a condition is one of a closed list, an effect is one of a closed list, and the game’s own interpreter reads them. A sites plugin may need card packs, as an island may (requires), and every card it names must be a card of the base game or of a pack it needs.

The game ships two, each a finished place and a study to read: An Old Adventurer’s Hut and the Conjuration Wizard Guild. They are walked through below.

The manifest

plugin.json is an island’s manifest (Your first plugin) with three differences:

FieldRule
kind"sites". Without it a plugin is an island ("island" is the default).
api_version2 or newer; a sites plugin declaring API 1 is refused. A game older than API 2 refuses every sites plugin: it names kind as a field it does not know.
regionNone. A sites plugin has no island to name, and a region refuses it.

Everything else is as for an island: id, name, version, authors, description, license, learning_note, requires, minimum_game_version, and the files list that export writes. The id begins every picture key and every button the game makes for the plugin, and saves keep the plugin’s progress under it: never change it. The Hut’s manifest:

A wrong manifest is named in one of these sentences:

  • plugin.json: kind must be one of island, sites.
  • plugin.json: a sites plugin needs api_version 2 or newer.
  • plugin.json: a sites plugin has no region; its places are described in sites.json.

Its files

A sites plugin’s content is two files:

  • sites.json
  • text.json

sites.json holds its places, their pictures and sprites, panes and services, its flags and its dungeons. text.json is the same file as an island’s: every sentence, title and button by key (Text and placeholders). Beside them go the pictures, a README.txt and a LICENSE.txt. An island’s files (world.json and the rest) are not read in a sites plugin. The package rules, the limits and the files list are an island’s (Testing).

A place

Where places stand

A place’s where takes either or both of:

  • mainland
  • islands

mainland puts one on Shandalar’s mainland. islands puts one on the island of every island plugin the campaign sails to. A place with both is one place in many spots: its uses are counted once for all of them.

  1. When. On the mainland, when the campaign begins; a place new to an adventure (a new place in an update of the plugin) the next time the adventure is loaded. On an island, the first time the hero lands there.
  2. Which tile. A land tile of the land the hero starts on (on an island, the port’s land): no town, road or dungeon on it, and at least gap tiles from every town, village, castle, dungeon, anchorage, harbor and other place. Next to a road when such a tile exists, so the hero walks past it.
  3. The dice. The tile is drawn on the plugin’s own dice, seeded from the campaign’s seed, the plugin’s salt and the place’s key. The same campaign seed puts it on the same tile every time. The adventure’s own dice are never touched: with a sites plugin ticked, the towns, dungeons, creatures and every draw of the game are the same as without it.
  4. Kept. Once placed, the tile is saved with the land and the place never moves. When no tile fits, the place does not stand on that land. A land holds at most 64 places.

On the map the place is drawn by its sprite and listed on the map and the minimap. Walking onto it opens its pane, which pushes the wandering creatures back as a town’s does. Its places stand while the plugin is ticked for the adventure, installed and valid, and every card pack it needs is one of the campaign’s, switched on and as new as the plugin asks.

Panes and their when

A place shows the first of its panes whose when holds. A when is a list of conditions that must all hold, and an empty one always holds. So list the special panes first and end with one that has no when. The Hut chooses its pane by its dungeon’s state: cleared, open, or not yet raised.

FieldRule
whenConditions, at most 16. Default [], which always holds.
titleA text key. Default: the place’s name.
subtitleA text key. Default: the plugin’s name from its manifest.
pictureA picture key. Default: the place’s picture.
linesAt most 16. Each a text key, or {"text": "key", "when": [...]}: a line said only while its conditions hold.
actionsThe pane’s buttons, at most 8 (Actions).

Actions

  • copy_card
  • leave
  • talk
  • duel
  • buy_card
  • sell_card

A button the hero cannot use now is shown grayed: they lack its price, or, for a copy, hold no card that may be copied or have no room in the bag. Should a press still fail, nothing is paid and the pane says why (site.no_gold, site.bag_full, site.no_cards, site.no_room).

One id, one action

The same action may stand in several panes, written the same in each, field for field. Two different actions with one id refuse the plugin, and so do two leave buttons with one id that differ in any field, a label say: sites.json: sites.shrine.panes[1].actions[1].id: leave names another action of this site; an id is one action (the same action may stand in several panes). Copy a button word for word into every pane, or give it another id.

Prices, and how an action is used up

The first use of an action costs its first price, the second use its second price, and so on. Once every price is paid, the action is used up: its button is gone from every pane, and the condition used_up holds for it. An action with no prices is free and never used up. A price of 0 is a free use that still counts.

Uses are counted for the plugin as a whole. The Guild’s tower stands on the mainland and on every island, and all its towers share one count: four copies in all, wherever they are made. One kind of action is counted on each land by itself: an action with prices that raises a dungeon, since each land has its own dungeon to sell. The Guild’s copy action:

Its label, Copy a card ({price} gold, {uses_left} left), reads “Copy a card (2000 gold, 4 left)” the first time and “(12000 gold, 1 left)” the last.

Flags

A flag is a switch of your own, declared in sites.json’s flags (at most 32). Every flag is off at the start of a campaign. Effects set and clear it, and conditions read it. A plugin’s flags are one set for the whole adventure, the same on every land. A flag a condition or effect names that flags does not declare refuses the plugin.

Dungeons

A dungeon of sites.json is a recipe that spawn_dungeon raises. It belongs to no quest. Its treasure is the cards the plugin lists.

FieldRule

sizeIts creatures’ size, default small.

The colors, sizes and rules:

  • black
  • blue
  • green
  • red
  • white
  • random
  • small
  • large
  • no_color
  • no_artifacts
  • no_instants
  • life_carries
  • life_adds

How one is raised. It rises on the land the hero stands on, on a land tile of the place’s own land where a dungeon may stand, at least min and at most max tiles from the hero. When no tile is within that distance, it takes one of the nearest tiles past min. The tile is drawn on the plugin’s dice. Its color, size, rules, cards and name are the recipe’s, and everything about it is known: its place on the map, its creatures and its rules, as if the hero had heard every clue.

Then it is an ordinary dungeon. The hero enters it, fights its creatures and takes its cards as in any dungeon. It is drawn on the map and listed with the others, and it moves when left, as 1997’s dungeons do. It belongs to the land, not to the plugin: if the plugin goes missing, the dungeon stays.

  • One dungeon of a key is open on a land at a time. Once it is cleared, a new spawn_dungeon of that key raises a new one, on a new draw. To sell a dungeon once, give its action one price, as the Hut does.
  • A land holds at most 48 castles and dungeons in all, the game’s own counted. When the land is full, or no tile fits, the action is refused with site.no_room and nothing is paid.
  • Put spawn_dungeon before a log_clue or log_event whose words name {dungeon}: the name is known only once the dungeon is raised.

Plugin API 3: more words for a place

Everything above is plugin API 2. API 3 adds the words below: counters, costs besides gold, talk panes, lines that change, rolls, duels, card trades and tasks. A plugin that uses one of them says "api_version": 3 in plugin.json, and then needs a game that reads API 3; an older game refuses it in one line and plays on without it. The tool’s commands set the number by themselves the moment a command adds such a word, and say so. Written by hand with the old number, the plugin is refused, naming the word and where it stands:

sites.json: sites.den.panes[0].actions[0].when[0].gold_below: "gold_below" is a word of plugin API 3, and plugin.json declares api_version 2. Set "api_version": 3 there (the plugin then needs a game that reads API 3).

The cheat sheet lists every word on one page, each with what to write and the command that writes it.

Counters

A counter is a number of the plugin’s own, from 0 to 1000, 0 at the start of a campaign and kept in the save. Declare it in sites.json’s counters; change it with the effect counter ({"name": "wins", "add": 1}, or {"name": "wins", "set": 0}); ask about it with the condition counter ({"name": "wins", "min": 3}); show it with {count}. The Riddle Stone counts wrong answers, and shows a hint once there are two:

{count} is the counter the action changes or its when asks about; in a line with a when of its own, the counter that line asks about; else the pane’s; else the plugin’s first.

Costs besides gold

needs is what an action takes besides its price, each time it is done. All of it is needed, and all of it is taken. While the hero lacks one, the button is grayed and nothing is paid; the pane says which (site.needs_card, site.needs_amulets, site.needs_any_amulets, site.needs_food).

The Collector’s trade shows only while the hero holds the card, takes one copy, and pays for it. Its three prices of 0 mean “three times, free”:

Something given needs an end

An action that gives a card (give_card) or amulets (amulet with a count above 0) must have prices or needs, or it is refused: without them a hero could press it without end. A list of prices ends, and a need must be found again each time. For gold given freely validate only warns.

Talk panes

A pane with an id is never chosen by itself. A talk action opens it, by its to. That is all a conversation needs: a question is a button, its answer is a pane, and a wrong answer leads to another pane. The pane a talk opens still needs its own Leave. A talk may carry prices, needs and effects, applied before the pane opens; a talk with none of them only turns the page, and is not counted as a use. The Stone’s right answer sets a flag and pays, once:

A place always needs a pane to open on: the last pane without an id must have no when. And every pane, named or not, needs a leave action without a when. A place without either is refused, since the hero could not get in, or out.

Lines that change

A line may be {"one_of": ["inn.rumor1", "inn.rumor2", "inn.rumor3"], "by": "day"}: one of 2 to 16 texts, in turn. With "by": "day" (the default) the next one each day of the journey; with "by": "uses" the next one each time an action of the place is done. No dice are cast, so the line is the same after a reload. It may have a when like any other line.

Rolls

The effect roll picks one of 2 to 6 outcomes by its weight (1 to 100, default 1), applies that outcome’s effects and shows its result lines after the action’s own. The Gambler’s Den:

The dice are the plugin’s own, drawn for this land, place, action and use: reloading the game gives the same throw, and the adventure’s own dice are never touched. An action has one roll at most; there is none inside a roll’s outcome or a duel’s win or lose. walk prints every outcome with its chance and the gold a throw brings on average, and validate warns when that is more than the cheapest price.

Duels and foes

A foes entry is an opponent: a name, life (1 to 99), a face (a creature row, as an island’s foes: Foes and rewards), a deck of 1 to 250 cards as {"card": copies}, and optionally a deck_name. The deck, life and face are fixed, never scaled to the hero. A duel action names one:

The price is the stake. It is paid as the duel begins, and a duel left unfinished is not saved: the adventure resumes with the stake paid. A draw is a loss. win and lose each have effects and result lines; without lines the game says site.duel_won or site.duel_lost. No ante is staked. {foe} and {life} are filled in the action’s label and lines. The hero needs a deck, as for any duel.

Card trades

buy_card sells the hero the cards of its cards, each at its price; sell_card buys those of its cards the hero holds. The hero picks from a card list whose numbers are the prices. With stock, that many of each card are traded in a campaign and no more.

{"id": "buy", "do": "buy_card", "cards": {"Jayemdae Tome": 300, "Rod of Ruin": 200}, "stock": 1}
{"id": "sell", "do": "sell_card", "cards": {"Glasses of Urza": 60}}

A place is no way to mint gold: the game never sells a card for less, nor buys one for more, than the most a town pays for it. The tool has no card values, so walk trades at the prices you wrote. A trade has no prices and no needs; its lines are result, by default site.bought and site.sold.

Tasks

A tasks entry is a row of the hero’s Tasks list, with a place on the map to guide them to:

"tasks": {"errand": {"title": "errand.task_title", "text": "errand.task_text", "done": "errand.task_done",
                     "at": "place:errand", "when": [{"flag": "errand_asked"}], "done_when": [{"flag": "errand_done"}]}}

The row is shown while when holds, and done, and no longer a map target, once done_when holds (never, when that is empty). at is place: and a place of this plugin, or dungeon: and one of its dungeons (once raised); a task at a place is shown only on a land the place stands on. In a task’s conditions used and used_up read the place named by at; without one, use site_used. A quest needs no machinery of its own: a talk that sets a flag, a need, a duel or a dungeon, and a task that shows the way.

Progress and saves

Save version 9 keeps a sites plugin’s progress, all of it under the plugin’s id:

  • hero.adventures: the plugin’s row, as an island’s, with "kind": "sites".
  • sites.id: uses, each action’s count by "place.action" (an action with prices that raises a dungeon is counted for each land, by "place.action@land"; a trade with a stock also counts each card traded); flags, the names set; counters, each counter that is not 0, by its name (API 3); dungeons, each dungeon raised by "land:dungeon".
  • Each land’s map keeps its places’ tiles (expansion.sites, by "plugin:place"), and a raised dungeon is part of its land like any dungeon.

A save from before version 9 opens with no sites progress. A plugin missing in the middle of a campaign: the save still opens, its places hide, and their tiles and the plugin’s progress are kept, so putting it back brings everything back. A dungeon it raised stays an ordinary dungeon. A sites plugin never has a ship or a note on the mainland, and its Tasks rows (API 3) go with its places, so nothing else changes. A card pack it needs switched off hides its places the same way.

Updates. Saves know a sites plugin by its id, a place by its key, an action by its id, a counter by its name and a raised dungeon by its key. Change one of them and what was saved under it is lost: a renamed action starts again from its first price. Everything else is safe: words, pictures, panes, conditions and effects are read fresh; a new place is placed on the next load; prices may change (a save’s uses so far are kept, so do not shorten a price list or give a free action prices: a save that used it more often finds it used up); a new salt, gap or where reaches only lands where the place is not placed yet. Compare every release with the one players have:

$ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py compat dist/old/my_places.zip dist/my_places.zip

For a sites plugin compat lists a place removed or renamed, an action id removed and a dungeon key removed, and exits 1. It also warns, and exits 1, when a save may find an action used up: a free action that now has prices (its free uses were counted too) and a price list made shorter. It warns of a counter renamed or removed too. It notes, and exits 0 for, a place added, a price list made longer or given other prices, an action made free, a dungeon recipe changed, a flag removed, and a new where, gap or salt.

Tools for places

The authoring tool builds a place with you, step by step. Each step is one command of tools/adventure_plugin.py, run in the game folder; on Windows type python and \ as on the first page. The loop:

  • new --kind sites
  • add-place
  • add-action
  • add-pane
  • add-dungeon
  • set-text
  • preview
  • walk
  • art
  • validate
  • the Lab
  • export

No JSON file needs to be opened for any of it: each command writes what it adds, checks the whole plugin as the game will, and changes nothing when the result would be refused. Each says what it did and which command comes next.

  1. Start a sites plugin

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py new my_places --id my_places --name "My Places" --kind sites

    It writes a sites plugin that loads and plays: one place, a wayside shrine on the mainland and on every island, whose service costs 20, 40 and 80 gold and is then used up, with a small stand-in picture and map sprite.

  2. Add a place

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-place my_places --id tower --name "Glass Tower" --where mainland,islands --service copy --prices 300,600

    add-place writes the place into sites.json: a pane with an intro line, the service and a Leave button, and for a priced service the pane shown once it is used up. Its words go into text.json as tower.*, ready to rewrite, and a stand-in pane picture and map sprite into art/, their picture key registered. --service is effects (the default: pay, then effects), copy (copy a card) or none; --where is mainland (the default), islands or both; --prices are the prices in turn. An id the plugin already has, or an island plugin, is refused in one line. The two JSON files are written again whole, tab-indented.

  3. Add a dungeon

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-dungeon my_places --id cellar --name "The Dry Cellar" --cards "Lightning Bolt,Llanowar Elves" --size large --place tower --price 250

    add-dungeon adds the recipe: --cards (1 to 6, joined by commas), --color, --size, --rules, --min and --max. With --place it also gives that place a button that raises the dungeon for --price gold (default 1000), and the Hut’s three panes: two new panes before the place’s others, for the dungeon cleared and open, with their words. At a place that already has a service both stay: the two new panes carry the place’s other buttons, and the dungeon’s button stands in every pane of the place’s own. --action names the button; name an effects action the place already has and spawn_dungeon is put first in its effects instead. The card names are checked as validate checks them, and an edit that would refuse the plugin changes nothing.

  4. Or start from a recipe

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-place my_places --id camp --name "The Collector's Camp" --recipe collector --card "Ornithopter"

    --recipe writes a whole place of one of twelve kinds (Recipes); --list-recipes prints them. --service is the older, plainer way: one button, and with --free a button that costs nothing and is never used up.

  5. Add buttons, panes, flags, counters, foes

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-counter my_places coins
    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-action my_places --place tower --id wish --prices 5,5,5 --effect counter=coins+1 --effect food=2 --label "Throw in {price} gold ({uses_left} left)" --result "You have thrown {count} coins."
    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-pane my_places --place tower --id cellar --line "Stairs lead down into the dark."
    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py add-action my_places --place tower --id descend --do talk --to cellar --label "Go down the stairs"

    add-action puts a button into a place: --do (effects, talk, duel, copy, buy, sell), --prices, any number of --effect, --needs and --when, a --label and a --result written as plain words. An --effect is gold=50, food=6, set_flag=told, counter=wins+1, amulet=red, give_card=Shivan Dragon, next_duel=life:5, spawn_dungeon=vault, reveal_map=place:4 or log_clue=the words; a --when is flag=told, counter=wins:3, has_card=Black Lotus, hero_color=red and so on, and "A | B" holds when one of the two does. A roll is two to six --outcome "WEIGHT | EFFECTS | WORDS". --pane names the pane (a number, an id, or all). add-pane adds a pane with an --id (a talk opens it) or a --when; add-flag and add-counter declare a name; add-foe reads an opponent’s deck from a text file, one card a line, like 4 Lightning Bolt. The cheat sheet has every form.

  6. Write the words

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py set-text my_places tower.intro "A tower of green glass, humming faintly."
    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py set-info my_places --description "A glass tower and what lies under it." --license "CC BY 4.0" --version 1.0.0

    set-text writes or changes one sentence of text.json; set-info sets the plugin’s name, description, license, authors and version in plugin.json. remove-place --id KEY takes a place out again, with its words, its pictures and whatever only it used.

  7. Walk it

    walk plays each place as text, without the game. It shows the pane with no gold, then presses every ready service in turn, clears each dungeon it raised, and shows the pane after each step: the words filled in, each button pressable (x) or not, and why not. The Hut:

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py walk expanded_adventure/Old_Adventurers_Hut

    The walk follows the game’s own rules for panes, conditions, prices and effects. A place that stands on the mainland and on the islands is walked once for each. It has no world: a dungeon always finds room, and the hero always holds one card to copy. --site KEY walks one place, --gold N sets the hero’s gold (default 100000; try a small sum to see what the hero cannot afford), --json prints the steps for your own scripts.

    8. A walk’s hero can be told what to carry and what to do: --gold, --food, --cards "A,B", --amulets red=2, --color, --day, --flag, --counter wins=3; --press a,b,c presses exactly those buttons of one --site (leave steps out and in again, clear clears the open dungeon); --land walks one land, --both sends one hero over the mainland and then an island; --magics "A,B", --life-bonus N and --linked say what the hero owns already; --win and --lose end every duel that way (a walk fights none); a teleport prints where the hero is carried; --roll N takes every roll’s N-th outcome. A roll is printed whole, each outcome with its chance and its gold, and the average. At its end a walk lists what it never reached — a pane never shown, a button never pressed — with what stood in the way and the option that opens it. Only a real error makes it fail.

  8. Preview it

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py preview my_places

    preview writes one HTML page into preview/ (or --out DIR; --site shows one place, --open opens the browser). It shows every pane of every place in the game’s proportions: the picture, the title, the lines with their placeholders filled for a sample hero, and under each pane, line and button a small caption of its when in plain words (“shown when: flag told, gold ≥ 500”). Each button says what it does: “pays 50 gold, 5 times in all; counter den_plays +1, rolls: 3 in 6 nothing, 2 in 6 +75 gold, 1 in 6 +150 gold”, and the words it then says. Below come the dungeons with their cards, the opponents with their decks, the Tasks rows and the warnings. The page asks the network for nothing and runs no script; a picture up to 400 KB is put into the page itself, a bigger one is named by its path. A plugin’s words are shown as words, never as markup.

  9. Read the warnings

    validate refuses what the game refuses, in the game’s words. Besides that it warns of what loads but is not finished or would surprise a player, and a warning never refuses: the tool’s stand-in sentences, license, description and pictures still in place; a flag, counter, text, picture, foe or dungeon nothing uses; a placeholder where it is not filled; {price} or {uses_left} in a free action’s label; a pane too long to fit (it does not scroll); a pane that can never be shown; a button that gives gold without end; a roll that pays more than it costs. When a line holds a sentence where a text key belongs, or a card’s name is mistyped, it says what to write instead.

  10. Look at the art

    art measures the pictures and gives advice. It never refuses a plugin. The Guild:

    $ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py art expanded_adventure/Conjuration_Wizard_Guild
Conjuration Wizard Guild (tower)
  pane picture  art/conjuration_guild_tower.png: 1024 x 560, 1.83 : 1 (the pane's frame is 512 x 280, 1.83 : 1)
  map sprite    art/conjuration_guild_map.png: 320 x 400, drawn 92 world pixels wide (a tile is 128 x 64)
    trimmed to 276 x 384 at 22, 13 (48% of the picture transparent); drawn 92 x 128; its foot in the middle
    3 source pixels to a world pixel: a whole number, so pixel art stays crisp.
0 pieces of advice. Advice only: art never refuses a plugin (validate does that).
    • The pane picture. The pane’s frame is 512 × 280, about 1.83 to 1, and the picture is cropped to fill it. Advice comes for another shape, and for a picture under 512 pixels wide.
    • The map sprite. Its transparent margins are trimmed, and the rest is drawn width world pixels wide; a tile is 128 × 64. Advice comes for a sprite with no transparency (its background would be drawn as a block), for a foot that is not in the middle of the bottom edge (the point that stands on the tile), and for a width over 128.
    • The scale. The source pixels to one world pixel. A whole number, as the Hut’s and the Guild’s 3, keeps pixel art crisp; otherwise art names the widths that would make it whole.

    A PNG’s transparency is read; for a JPEG, a WebP or an unusual PNG art says what it did not check. It reads an island plugin’s port and castle the same way.

  11. Validate, the Lab, export

    validate, info, export and compat work on a sites plugin as on an island (Testing). info lists its places, where they stand, their actions and its dungeons. A sites problem names the place in sites.json, for example sites.json: sites.shrine.panes[1].actions[0].effects[2].spawn_dungeon: no dungeon vault in sites.json dungeons.

    In a source checkout, ./tools/expansion_lab.sh --out lab-my_places --plugin my_places then plays the plugin in the game itself, headless, for each seed (Testing): it places the places on a fresh mainland and on a small test island, opens each pane and presses every button it offers with gold enough, checks every dungeon raised, saves and reads the adventure back, and takes the plugin away to see its places hide and its progress kept.

    Last, in the game: put the folder in the plugins folder, start a new adventure, tick the plugin in Expanded adventure plugins... and embark. A place on the mainland stands near a road; open the map to find it. For a place on the islands, tick an island plugin too and sail there. After a change to words, panes or prices, Rescan or start the game again and load your save; a place is placed once, so a new salt, gap or where shows in a new adventure. Then export the zip to share.
