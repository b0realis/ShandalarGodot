Saves and compatibility

Players keep their adventures for months. This page says what a save holds of your plugin, what happens when the plugin is missing or changed, and what never to change once players have it.

What a save holds

  • hero.adventures: the plugins ticked at the Embark, each {"id", "version", "name"}. The name is kept so the game can name a plugin that has gone missing.
  • expansions.id: the progress record, quests (the indices done), stages ({"0": 1}: quest index to steps done), reward_choices, pending (cards waiting) and each flag you declared.
  • regions.id: the island’s map while the hero is elsewhere. While the hero is on the island, it is the save’s world itself, and the mainland waits in regions.mainland. The island’s map data keeps its port and castle, its town names, its chosen objectives and its pennants.

When a plugin is on

A plugin’s ships sail for a campaign while it was ticked at that campaign’s Embark, is installed and valid, and every card pack it needs is one of the campaign’s, enabled, and at least the min_version the plugin asks for. When one of those fails, the ship does not sail and, once a launch, the mainland says why in one of these sentences:

  • “Ships to The Last Refuge do not sail: the plugin … is not installed.”
  • “Ships to … do not sail: the plugin … is refused.”
  • “Ships to … do not sail: the plugin … needs Pack 2 · Fallen Empires.”
  • “Ships to … do not sail: the plugin … needs Pack 2 · Fallen Empires 1.1 or newer (installed 1.0).”
  • “Ships to … do not sail: this adventure was saved with version 1.0.0 of the plugin …, and the installed version 1.1.0 cannot read it.”

A plugin missing in the middle of a campaign

The save still opens. The land’s progress and map are kept exactly as they were, so putting the plugin back brings everything back. A hero standing on the island finds the game’s own Ship home pane at its port, and a Leave pane at its castle: the ship always takes the hero home. A missing card pack is different: an adventure whose campaign packs are missing still cannot be resumed, as without plugins.

A plugin changed under a save

When a player installs a newer version of your plugin, each saved land is read against it:

The saved land isWhenWhat the game does
fineIt fits the installed version.Plays on. The saved version in hero.adventures is brought up to the installed one when the save loads.
changedWhole, but not what the installed version describes: a castle or town slot it asks for is missing from the saved map, a quest index, done count, stage or number of waiting cards is beyond what it counts, or the finale’s flag is on before every quest is done.Keeps the land. It is carried exactly as saved, its ships do not sail and it has no task or voyage rows; a hero on it finds Ship home and Leave panes that name the saved version and ask for it back. The mainland note says the same. Putting the old version back restores the land on the next load.
damagedBroken under any version: its map, region id, palette, names, objectives, pennants or anchorages malformed, the land without its port, its map not 128 site records, or its progress the wrong shape.The save is refused, as any damaged save is.

So the game keeps the land and lets the hero sail home, but a player who updated and cannot play your island is still a player let down. Keep your updates readable by old saves.

What never to change once players have it

ChangeEffect on saves
The idnever A new id is a new plugin: old saves keep the old id’s land, carried and not sailing. For a different campaign, use a new id.
Removing or reordering questsnever Quests are saved by their index; a removed one leaves a done count or index the new version does not have, and a moved one hands one quest’s progress to another.
Shortening a questnever A saved stage past the new last step does not fit. Inserting a step moves the meaning of every saved stage after it.
Adding, moving or removing a town slotnever Islands already made do not have the new slot, so they no longer fit.
Appending a questcareful Fine, unless the finale needs "quests_done": "all": a save that already won the finale then has its flag on with a quest open.
Renaming or removing a flagcareful The old value stays in saves, unread; the new flag starts false.
Everything else in world.json: places, villages, names, the dungeon, salt, dice_saltsafe Reaches only islands made after the change. A renamed town keeps its old name on an island already made, while its quest panes use the new one.
Words, foes’ decks and life, rewards, panes, talk, pictures, music, the palettesafe Read fresh every time.
A new required card packcareful Campaigns that did not tick that pack can no longer sail; the mainland says which pack is needed.

Bump version with every release, and world.json’s layout_version (saved with each island) when the layout changes. Before every release, compare it with the one players have:

$ python3 tools/adventure_plugin.py compat dist/old/my_isle.zip dist/my_isle.zip
# Windows, in the same folder: python tools\adventure_plugin.py compat dist\old\my_isle.zip dist\my_isle.zip

compat lists every change that would hold an old save’s land: the id, a town slot added, moved or removed, a quest removed, moved or shortened, a quest appended while the finale needs every quest, the markers. It exits 1 with that list. What old saves carry without harm it prints as notes and exits 0: a place added, moved or removed, a flag renamed or removed, a step that changed kind, and recipe changes that only alter islands not yet made.

Versions the game checks

  • api_version newer than the game’s plugin API: refused, “a newer game is needed”.
  • minimum_game_version newer than the game: refused, naming both versions.
  • A card pack in requires the game does not know: refused.
