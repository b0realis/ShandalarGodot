Quests and steps

A quest is an ordered list of steps. The number the save keeps for it is how many steps are done, and the quest is done when its last step, a duel, is won.

Steps

A step’s do is one of:

  • accept
  • go
  • duel
StepFieldsWhat the hero does
acceptaction (optional; the button, default quest.accept)Presses Accept in the quest’s pane at its giver. Only a quest’s first step may be an accept.
goto, action, guidance, task (all required), lineWalks to the target and presses the step’s button there. The button appears in the target’s pane; line is the words beside it at a town (the guidance at a marker).
duelat (default the giver), guidance, task, approachWins a duel against the quest’s foe at the target. Always the last step. Away from the giver, approach gives the button and line there that open the quest’s pane with its Duel button.

guidance is the line in the quest’s pane and the clue in the journal while the step is current; task is the Tasks list’s objective. Without them the engine’s words say it (guide.petition, guide.duel, task.petition, task.duel; Text).

Targets

A step’s to, at and the here condition take one of:

  • "giver", "port" or "castle";
  • {"site": 22}, a town or place slot of world.json;
  • {"objective": "channel", "else": "port"}, the site a named objective chose, or else (default giver) when it chose none.

At the giver town

A town that gives a quest shows town.line and, in the wise man’s place, a button labeled town.action (town.thanks once the quest is done). The town’s shops and services stay. The button opens the quest’s pane: the petition (the epilogue once done), the optional quest.reward_line, the step’s guidance, quest.retry_line, the reason a duel cannot begin yet if there is one, and the step’s button: Accept at stage 0 of a quest that begins with accept, else Duel, live when the duel is the current step, the hero stands at its target and the deck can play.

errands_off lists the town slots that offer none of the mainland’s ordinary errands; the Refuge lists slots 0 to 5, so its five communities offer none.

Conditions

A condition is an object with exactly one of these keys. Wherever a list of them stands (a pane’s, a line’s or a button’s when, the finale’s conditions), every one must hold; an empty list always holds. At most 16 in a list.

  • flag
  • not_flag
  • stage
  • quest_done
  • quests_done
  • deck_share
  • on_region
  • here
  • rewards_owed
  • cards_pending
ConditionExample valueHolds when
flag"liberated"The flag is set.
not_flag"liberated"The flag is not set.
stage{"quest": 1, "min": 1, "max": 2}Quest 1’s stage is within min and max (defaults 0 and 8).
quest_done3Quest 3 is done.
quests_done"all" or 3Every quest is done, or at least that many.
deck_share{"pack": "pack-2", "percent": 50}At least that percent of the main deck’s nonland cards are cards of the pack (one the manifest requires). Every card must be known and there must be at least one nonland card.
on_regiontrueThe hero is (true) or is not (false) on this island.
here"castle"The hero stands at the target: port, castle, a site or an objective (a giver means nothing outside a quest).
rewards_owedtrueA reward choice is (true) or is not (false) owed.
cards_pendingtrueReward cards are (true) or are not (false) waiting for room in the bag.

Quests are named by their place in quests.json’s list, from 0.

Effects

An effect is an object with exactly one of these keys, run in order. Effects run when a quest is done (its complete) and when the finale is won (its effects). At most 16 in a list. None of them touches the card packs.

  • set_stage
  • set_flag
  • gold
  • food
  • reward_choice
  • claim_cards
  • log_clue
  • log_event
  • refresh_appearance
EffectExample valueWhat it does
set_stage{"quest": 1, "value": 2}Sets a quest’s stage, at most that quest’s steps less one; the quest’s new guidance is logged as a quest clue.
set_flag"liberated"Sets the flag. No effect clears a flag, and only the finale’s effects may set the finale’s done_flag.
gold40Gold to the hero, from −100,000 to 100,000; it never falls below 0.
food8Food, from −1,000 to 1,000; never below 0.
reward_choicetrueOwes one reward choice for the quest being completed: at most one in a quest’s completion, and none in the finale’s effects.
claim_cardstrueMoves waiting reward cards into the bag while it has room.
log_clue{"text": "key", "quest": true}A clue in the journal (a quest clue when quest is true).
log_event{"event": "quest", "text": "key", "done": true}A line in the journal; event is a word of your own.
refresh_appearancetrueRedraws the island’s pennants from its progress: quest banners and marker banners.

Flags

A flag is a switch of your own, declared in quests.json’s flags (at most 8) and saved with the island’s progress, false until an effect sets it. A flag is copied into the island’s map data whenever its appearance is refreshed, so its name may not be one of the names the game already uses there or in the progress record:

  • region
  • layout_version
  • port
  • castle
  • town_names
  • dungeon_names
  • objectives
  • banners
  • freed_communities

The finale

at
castle (the default) or port: the marker whose boss button starts the duel.
foe, conditions
The duel, and what must hold for its button to be live (and still hold when it is won).
effects, done_flag
What a win does, and the flag that says the finale is won; only these effects may set it. When the conditions include "quests_done": "all", the game also holds a saved done_flag to it: a save with it set and a quest still open does not fit the plugin (Saves and compatibility).
won, already, unmet
The words after the duel: won and freed; won again after the finale was already done; won while the conditions did not hold.

The deck gate counts the main deck’s nonland cards of the named pack against all its nonland cards; lands and the sideboard never count. The castle can show the count with the deck gate’s placeholders (Text): the Refuge’s castle says Pledges: {pledges} of {quests_total}. Your main deck: {count} of {total} nonland cards are from Fallen Empires.

Guidance, status and tasks

targets
Where the guide arrow points once every quest is done (after_quests, default the castle) and once the finale’s flag is set (after_finale, default the port).
status
Lines for the world screen’s status while the hero is on the island; each a text key or {"text": key, "when": [...]}.
deck_line
A text key shown in the town deck builder on the island, filled with the deck gate’s numbers.
tasks
The Tasks list: a row per quest (its task words, or the engine’s), a row for the finale (tasks.finale: title, text, done), and, once a quest is done and the plugin has reward bundles, a row for the rewards (tasks.rewards). The rows are read from the save, so they stay right while the hero is on the mainland.
talk
What the island’s people say: news and applause (the wise man), roaming and tribute (a roaming creature, before and after enough wins), dungeon (the dungeon’s pane), tales (the wise man’s stories: title and text). News and applause show the first of their lines whose conditions hold; roaming and tribute show every one that holds.
pictures
The quest, rewards and result panes’ pictures; default the skin’s visit_city.
