Make a land for Shandalar

An adventure plugin is a small package of JSON, text, pictures and music that adds an island to the Shandalar adventure, reached by ship from a mainland harbor. This guide shows how one is made, tested and shared.

What a plugin is

A plugin is a .zip file, or an unpacked folder, with plugin.json at its top. Beside the manifest it holds the island: six content files, and pictures and music if you like. Players drop it into their expanded_adventure folder, open Expanded adventure plugins... at the bottom of the card-pack list on the Embark screen, and tick it. When their campaign begins, a ship waits at an anchorage on the mainland’s coast.

A plugin may name the card packs it needs. Ticking the plugin ticks those packs. The packs’ cards behave exactly as they do without the plugin: a plugin never withholds a card, unlocks one, or changes what one does.

The safety rule

Data only, never code. The game never loads, runs or mounts anything from a plugin. It reads the files as bytes, checks them, and decodes pictures and music from those bytes in memory. A quest is a list of steps; a condition is one of a closed list; an effect is one of a closed list. Every behavior is the game’s own interpreter reading your data. A .gd, .tscn, .tres, .pck, .dll or .exe, or any other kind of file not on the list, refuses the whole plugin.

The Last Refuge: a learning demo

The game ships one plugin, Fallen_Empires_Refuge_prototype.zip: The Last Refuge, an island of Sarpadian refugees for players with card pack 2, Fallen Empires. It is a game-design and learning demo, finished and playable, made so that you can play it, read it and take it apart. Its own manifest says so:

The community makes the lands

The game adds no adventure content of its own beyond that demo. New islands, their people, quests and foes are for players to make and share, at their will. This guide, the authoring tool and the game’s checks are here to make that easy and safe.

What a plugin can do in API 1

  • One island, cut from the game’s own terrain generator and laid out from your recipe: a port, a castle, towns, places, villages, roads and a dungeon.
  • Up to 12 quests, each a list of steps (accept, go, duel), with rewards when done, and a finale at the castle or the port.
  • Foes with fixed decks and life, roaming decks for the island’s creatures, reward bundles of cards.
  • Every word in text.json, pictures for the port and castle panes and their map sprites, and one music track for walking the island.

It cannot change the mainland (it touches only the anchorage, the voyage and the Tasks list), add cards, or run anything. Text is English only: text.json; the name text.<language>.json is kept for later versions.

Where things are

WhatWhere
Your pluginsThe folder Options names on its Adventure plugins folder line (the expanded_adventure_folder key in settings.cfg moves it). By default expanded_adventure in the game’s user folder: ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Shandalar/ on Linux, %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Shandalar\ on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Shandalar/ on macOS.
The game’s ownexpanded_adventure/ beside the game: the demo’s zip, a README and this guide.
In the gameHelp has an Adventure plugins page for players: what plugins are, where the folder is, how to tick one, and where this guide is.
The tooltools/adventure_plugin.py in the game folder (Python 3, nothing else to install).
The schemagame/adventure/plugins/schema_v1.json: the one description of the content files that both the game and the tool read. The Reference page is made from it.
