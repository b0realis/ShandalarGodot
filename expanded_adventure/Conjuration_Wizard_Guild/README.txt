Conjuration Wizard Guild
========================

A sites plugin for Shandalar: a wizards' tower that copies your cards.

THE PLACE
Behind a low wall stands a tower of grey stone, its high windows bright
with conjured light. The guild's conjurers copy any card you carry. The
first copy costs 2000 gold, the second 4000, the third 8000 and the fourth
12000; after that the guild's law allows no more. A copy follows the card
rules of your adventure (under the Classic MTG rules, never past the copies
they allow) and needs room in your bag.

The guild has a tower on the mainland and one on every island you sail to;
they keep one count between them.

WHAT YOU NEED
Nothing but the game: the plugin needs no card pack. At the Embark, open
"Expanded adventure plugins..." and tick it.

A SELF-CONTAINED PLACE, AND A STUDY
The guild is a finished place, and a study for the places players wish for:
one place on the mainland and on the islands, and a service whose price
climbs and then ends. Its fiction is original, not official Magic canon.

THE FILES (each is described in the guide, GUIDE/sites.html)
  plugin.json   the manifest: kind "sites", id, name, version
  sites.json    the tower: its pictures, map sprite, panes and service
  text.json     every sentence, title and button
  art/          the tower's picture and its map sprite
