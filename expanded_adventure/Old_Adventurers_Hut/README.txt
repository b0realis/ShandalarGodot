An Old Adventurer's Hut
=======================

A sites plugin for Shandalar: a new place in the mainland's woods.

THE PLACE
Smoke curls from a hut half swallowed by the trees. The very old man inside
was a planeswalker once, from another world, where he played at Shandalar
until the game drew him through. Now he wants only peace. For 1000 gold he
tells you where a dungeon lies that nobody else knows of: the Forgotten
Vault, which rises somewhere in the land around his hut, its guardians and
rules known to you. Its treasure is his old cards.

Clear it and come back: he knows of another, and tells you for 1000 gold
more. He has five secrets in all, sold one after another, each only once
the dungeon before it is cleared, and each lying farther from his hut
than the last:

  The Forgotten Vault      Old Man of the Sea, Planar Gate, Mirror Universe
  The Drowned Library      Library of Alexandria, Candelabra of Tawnos,
                           Transmute Artifact
  The Sunken Forge         Mishra's Workshop, Su-Chi, Triskelion
  The Hall of Echoes       Fork, Wheel of Fortune, Mana Flare
  The Planeswalker's Rest  The Abyss, Moat, Nether Void

While a dungeon of his stands uncleared he sells nothing. A campaign that
cleared the Vault with an older version of the hut finds him ready with
the second secret.

WHAT YOU NEED
Nothing but the game: the plugin needs no card pack. At the Embark, open
"Expanded adventure plugins..." and tick it. The hut stands near a road of
the mainland.

A SELF-CONTAINED PLACE, AND A STUDY
The hut is a finished place, and a study for the places players wish for:
one place, a chain of panes that each wait for the dungeon before them to
be cleared, a price for each secret, and five dungeons raised from the
plugin's own card lists. Its fiction is original, not official Magic canon.

THE FILES (each is described in the guide, GUIDE/sites.html)
  plugin.json   the manifest: kind "sites", id, name, version
  sites.json    the hut: its pictures, map sprite, panes and services, and
                the five dungeons its secrets raise
  text.json     every sentence, title and button
  art/          the hut's picture and its map sprite
