Pack 7 — Fifth Edition (7-5ED)

Why Fifth Edition?

Fifth Edition (1997) is the core set of Shandalar's own year. It gathers
the cards a 1997 player met at the table: the familiar core pool, and 147
cards that had first appeared in Ice Age, Homelands and Fallen Empires.
Every card already has a script in the game or in Packs 2 to 4, so the
pack adds no new rules identity. It adds the Fifth Edition printings with
their artwork, four illustrations for each basic land, and a Fifth Edition
source for the 147 expansion cards it reprints.

Fifth Edition: 434 names / 449 original English printings.
Common 185, uncommon 132, rare 132. The five † misprint variants and the
foreign printings are not part of the pack. Use Card variant in the Deck
Builder to choose and save artwork. Current Oracle rules apply.

Pack revision 1.0.0 requires Shandalar 0.40.13 or later. The set has its
own Extras filter in the Deck Builder. Decks that use a card from Ice
Age, Homelands or Fallen Empires can be played with either the original
pack or Pack 7 installed.

Build locally with tools/pack_7_fifth_edition.py. Artwork and the finished
ZIP are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-7-Fifth-Edition.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in
Options.
