Pack 10 — The Urza Block (10-USG)

Why the Urza block?

Urza's Saga (October 1998), Urza's Legacy (February 1999) and Urza's
Destiny (June 1999) go back in time to the planeswalker Urza after the
Brothers' War: the Tolarian Academy and its experiments with time, Serra's
Realm, and the long preparations against Phyrexia that become the Legacy
the Weatherlight will carry. The block brought echo, cycling, free spells
that untap the lands that paid for them and a host of artifacts and
enchantments. The pack brings the whole block as one optional expansion.

Urza's Saga: 335 names / 350 original English printings.
Urza's Legacy: 143 names / 143 original English printings.
Urza's Destiny: 143 names / 143 original English printings.
Together: 621 distinct names / 636 printings, including 591 new
identities. No name repeats across the three sets. Urza's Saga has four
illustrations for each basic land; use Card variant in the Deck Builder
to choose and save artwork. Foreign printings are not part of the pack.
Current Oracle rules apply.

The 30 cards the game already has keep their existing scripts. The five
basic lands and seven more are in the original 1997 pool; the other 18
use the rules script of an Ice Age, Portal, Portal Second Age or Mirage
card, and a deck that uses one of them can be played with any pack that
provides it installed, Pack 10 included.

Pack revision 1.0.0 requires Shandalar 0.60.19 or later. Each of the
three sets has its own Extras filter in the Deck Builder.

Build locally with tools/pack_10_urza_block.py. Artwork and the finished
ZIP are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-10-Urza-Block.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in
Options.
