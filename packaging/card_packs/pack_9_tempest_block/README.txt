Pack 9 — The Tempest Block (9-TMP)

Why the Tempest block?

Tempest (October 1997), Stronghold (March 1998) and Exodus (June 1998)
follow the skyship Weatherlight on from the Mirage block: its crew is
carried to Rath, the artificial plane ruled by the Evincar Volrath, to
rescue Captain Sisay, storms his stronghold and escapes. The block brought
shadow creatures, buyback spells, licids, slivers and spikes. The pack
brings the whole block as one optional expansion.

Tempest: 335 names / 350 original English printings.
Stronghold: 143 names / 143 original English printings.
Exodus: 143 names / 143 original English printings.
Together: 621 distinct names / 636 printings, including 574 new
identities. No name repeats across the three sets. Tempest has four
illustrations for each basic land; use Card variant in the Deck Builder
to choose and save artwork. Foreign printings are not part of the pack.
Current Oracle rules apply.

The 42 cards the game already has keep their existing scripts. Fifteen are
in the original 1997 pool; the other 27 use the rules script of an Ice
Age, Portal, Portal Second Age or Mirage card, and a deck that uses one of
them can be played with any pack that provides it installed, Pack 9
included.

Pack revision 1.0.0 requires Shandalar 0.50.15 or later. Each of the
three sets has its own Extras filter in the Deck Builder.

Build locally with tools/pack_9_tempest_block.py. Artwork and the finished
ZIP are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-9-Tempest-Block.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in
Options.
