Pack 8 — The Mirage Block (8-MIR)

Why the Mirage block?

Mirage (October 1996), Visions (February 1997) and Weatherlight (June
1997) are the expansions a 1997 Shandalar player was opening at the
table: the desert continent of Jamuraa, its djinn and efreet, flanking
knights and phasing creatures, and the first voyage of the skyship
Weatherlight. The pack brings the whole block as one optional expansion.

Mirage: 335 names / 350 original English printings.
Visions: 167 names / 167 original English printings.
Weatherlight: 167 names / 167 original English printings.
Together: 669 distinct names / 684 printings, including 621 new
identities. No name repeats across the three sets. Mirage has four
illustrations for each basic land; use Card variant in the Deck Builder
to choose and save artwork. The three † variant printings of Mirage and
the foreign printings are not part of the pack. Current Oracle rules apply.

The 43 cards the game already has keep their existing scripts. Twelve are
in the original 1997 pool; the other 31 first appeared in Ice Age,
Homelands, Portal or Portal Second Age, and a deck that uses one of them
can be played with either that pack or Pack 8 installed.

Pack revision 1.0.0 requires Shandalar 0.50.11 or later. Each of the
three sets has its own Extras filter in the Deck Builder.

Build locally with tools/pack_8_mirage_block.py. Artwork and the finished
ZIP are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-8-Mirage-Block.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in
Options.
