Pack 6 — Portal (1997)

200 named cards from the original English 215-card checklist.
27 reprints; 173 new rules identities. Original illustrations and full-card
scans preserve Portal’s large basic-land mana symbols. All 215 printings
are included, with four illustrations for each basic land. Use Card variant
in the Deck Builder to choose and save artwork. Current Oracle rules apply.

Build locally with tools/pack_6_portal.py. Artwork and the finished ZIP
are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-6-Portal.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in Options.
