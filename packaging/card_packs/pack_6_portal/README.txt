Pack 6 — Portal & Second Age

Why Portal?

We included the first Portal set for its beautiful illustrations and
distinctive card design, especially the basic lands with their large mana
symbols. Its straightforward creatures and spells can also offer new
players a gentler introduction to Magic and Shandalar.

Try the Portal starter decks and their illustrated guides in Help >
Contents > Portal starter decks, then explore the wider card pool at your
own pace. The game uses its normal rules, not a separate Portal ruleset.

Portal: 200 names / 215 original English printings.
Portal Second Age: 155 names / 165 original English printings.
Together: 318 distinct names / 380 printings, including 290 new identities.
Full-card scans preserve the large basic-land mana symbols. Portal has four
illustrations per basic land; Second Age has three. Use Card variant in the
Deck Builder to choose and save artwork. Current Oracle rules apply.

Pack revision 2.0.0 requires Shandalar 0.40.9 or later. Rebuild old Pack 6
ZIPs with the matching tool. The filename and saved decks are unchanged.
Each set has independent Extras filters. Seven original Second Age decks
and illustrated Help guides accompany the set in the game.

Build locally with tools/pack_6_portal.py. Artwork and the finished ZIP
are for local use and are not included in the source repository.
Place Pack-6-Portal.zip in the Card Packs folder and Rescan in Options.
