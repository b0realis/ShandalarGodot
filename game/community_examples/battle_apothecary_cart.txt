Battle Apothecary Cart - a study example

This card is a study example of a community card, not part of any official
set. Its zip shows how one is laid out: one folder named after the card,
holding card.json (the card itself), art.jpg (its picture) and its set
icon for each rarity: icon_common.png, icon_uncommon.png, icon_rare.png
and icon_legendary.png - one wheel drawn white with a black outline,
colored white, silver, gold and purple. Study it, change it, make your own.

Set: Wheel of Fortune. Art: Caduceus.
