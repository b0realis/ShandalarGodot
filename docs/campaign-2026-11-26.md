## What held

About 3,000 damaged saves and 21,800 damaged plugin files gave no script
error. Thirty duels in a row and repeated opening of the heavy screens
left object counts flat. About 90,000 hostile LAN and referee commands
gave no error. All 3,684 cards driven with a human seat: no decision
taken for the player. 244 sampled cards agree with their text. 1,952
random-deck duels: no stall. 360 town visits and 605 buys balanced.

## Known narrowings

- Card Packs: with ten packs on, Enable/Disable is still a 130–175 ms
  press and Rescan one 0.3–0.5 s frame, under the hourglass.
- The Deck Builder's "enable this pack and load" popup still rebuilds
  the pool in the foreground.
- The first opening of Help in a session still builds its pages inside
  the click (about 0.9 s).
- A page of card faces never shown before costs up to 30 ms to draw.
- Magic Battle, the gauntlet, LAN and the Deck Lab read deck files
  strictly; only the Deck Builder's Paste, Import and Load are forgiving.
- The computer still will not spend Triskelion's counters for lethal (a
  standing ruling).
- A file chooser's name field has no on-screen keyboard; Android keeps
  the system's file picker; rebinding a key needs a keyboard.
- Not run on a device or in a browser: the web Embark fix, the Android
  pause save, pad support on the Deck, the Quest and Windows.
