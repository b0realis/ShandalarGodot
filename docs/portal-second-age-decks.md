# Portal Second Age decks

Seven historical lists accompany the set in Pack 6. The five theme decks
contain 40 cards each; the two-player starters contain 30 each. Counts are
unchanged, with no sideboards, substitutions or invented extra cards.

Lists checked 2026-09-24 against the linked historical product catalogue.
In Magic Battle, look under **Portal Second Age decks**. Enable Pack 6 first.
**Help → Contents → Portal Second Age** contains original illustrated
guidance for all seven decks, not quotations from a commercial guide.

The 30-card starters work in casual local/hotseat/LAN duels with the existing
small-deck warning. Gauntlets and tournaments still require at least 40.
No 40-card adaptations of these starters are included: the five original
theme decks already provide 40-card beginner options.

Every name selects its P02 printing by default. The printed products mixed
three basic-land illustrations; the game's current deck format stores one
chosen illustration per name, so the artwork mix is not reproduced copy
by copy. Counts are unaffected. Normal shuffling replaces any prearranged
teaching order, and current rules replace obsolete starter wording.
Separate demo-game packs and alternate instructional card faces are not
included. Original Portal's existing starters and adaptations are unchanged.

Verification: exact quantities and printing preferences for all seven lists
are pinned by `tests/ui/test_second_age_decks.gd`. All seven participated in
the fourteen completed Wizard duels recorded in the [pack guide](pack-6-portal.md).
