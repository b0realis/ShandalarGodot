## #12 — Dungeon Generation and Treasure Allocation (answered, closed)

**Report** (0.60.16, Windows). The Ruins of Su Chan, the player's first
dungeon, held one treasure card (Cyclopean Tomb) and two bags of gold and
amulets; they expected two or three cards and one bag.

**Finding.** The 1997 placement, which the world generator keeps exactly
(`WorldGenerator`, the 28 `ShandalarWorld.DUNGEON_CARDS`): each card goes to
one of the ten dungeons at random, three places each, so 30 places hold 28
cards and a dungeon may hold 3, 2 or 1. Every dead end is a treasure room
(`DungeonBoard`): a card while the dungeon holds one, gold and amulets
after. Over 300 worlds the generator made, 84% of dungeons held three cards,
12% two and 4% one; 39% of worlds had a one-card dungeon. A dungeon's clue
page shows its cards. No change; the owner chose to explain and close.

## #13 — Spirit Link life at the end of a dungeon duel (fixed by a setting)

**Report.** At 46 life in the Cave of the Ice Worm (life carries from room
to room), a Juggernaut wearing two Spirit Links dealt the killing blow; the
hero left at 46, not 56.

**Cause.** Under the modern rules, the default, the duel ends the moment the
creature's life reaches 0, as a state-based action, before the two Spirit
Link triggers of that blow resolve. With the 1997 rules fork
`life_checked_at_phase_end` (Options → Rules, "Negative life is
survivable") the creature lives until combat ends, the gains resolve, and
the hero leaves at 56.

**The owner's ruling:** *"Add this setting to campaign settings and explain
it!"* **Fix.** A Campaign Settings row, **Life at 0**: a box, **Negative
life is survivable**, off for a new adventure, ticked by As 1997. Ticked,
every duel of the adventure plays the 1997 life check whatever Options →
Rules says (`AdventurePlayer.life_checked_at_phase_end`,
`CreatureMeeting.campaign_rules`, `DuelConfig.rule_forks`,
`DuelOpening.prepare`); unticked, the Options decide. Its line, tooltip and
the Help's Campaign Settings page explain it. Saved in the hero's record
(absent = off). An adventure begun before it keeps Options → Rules as its
way to the rule.

**Tests.** `tests/unit/test_issue13_negative_life.gd` (both rules on the
reporter's blow, every kind of adventure duel's config, the opening's
forks, the save, the Embark's memory);
`tests/ui/test_c19_A1_campaign_settings.gd` (the row, As 1997, the keys,
the window's fit).

## #16 — Battle instantly ending (open, waiting on the reporter)

**Report** (2026-11-12, "the latest build", a saved game). Every duel, on
the map and in dungeons, is won at once: the victory music before the coin
toss, then "You won!".

**What the video shows.** The duel screen carries the fifth button under the
large card (the Don't auto tap marks of 2026-11-11), so the build is the
unreleased 0.60.19 `main`, built by the reporter. The opponent, the
Enchantress in the Dungeons of Graaz Keep, starts with 0 cards in its library
and 0 in hand (the hero 32 and 7; a Marsh Viper stands on its side of the
table), so it loses at its first draw. Generic duels pass in CI, so the cause
lies in the reporter's own save, settings, packs or build.

**Asked** (2026-11-13): whether a brand-new adventure does it too (if not, a
new save is the way to play on); the `duel_log.txt`, the newest `godot*.log`
and the adventure's Export zip; the commit built and the Godot version.
Any save that arrives is untrusted data: read as JSON with the game's own
field-by-field reader in a scratch profile, never imported into a real one.

**The likely cause** (posted the same day, waiting on the reporter's word):
a build exported from the Godot editor without the project's presets.
`export_presets.cfg` is ignored; the tracked `export_presets.cfg.example`
(CONTRIBUTING: copy it before exporting) gives every preset
`include_filter="*.deck, *.txt, cards/data/*.json, packaging/card_packs/*/*.json"`.
A preset made in the editor has none, and Godot exports only its resources
and the files a filter names: the 55 creatures' decks
(`decks/1997/originals/*.deck`, read by `CreatureDeck` with `FileAccess`) are
left out, the Centaur Shaman's fallback with them, so `CreatureDeck.for_meeting`
returns no card and every creature loses at its first draw. The hero's deck
comes from the save, so it plays. Running the project in the editor reads
`res://` whole and is unaffected.
