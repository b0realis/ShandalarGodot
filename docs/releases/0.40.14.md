# Shandalar 0.40.14

- Two lured attackers at once no longer lock the declare-blockers step.
  "All creatures able to block it do so" (Lure, Elvish Bard, Marble
  Priest's own clause) is a requirement, and a blocker that cannot obey two of
  them obeys as many as it can (CR 509.1c): once it is blocking lured
  attackers up to its allowance, the other lure is not asked of it. Two
  Elvish Bards attacking together used to make the engine refuse every
  declaration, the empty one included — the AI conceded on the spot and a
  human seat could never have left the step. A creature that may block
  two (Two-Headed Giant of Foriys) still takes both lures.
- Found by an all-packs engine sweep: every printing of all seven packs
  from the real archives and every one of the 1,898 cards played through
  eight AI turns under both rulesets, with this the only fault.

No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
