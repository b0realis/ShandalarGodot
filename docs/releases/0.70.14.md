# Shandalar 0.70.14

More of the computer's play, from the same player's report.

## The computer's spells

- **Great Defender and Blood Lust are no longer cast on your creature.** The computer aims them at its own side and saves them for a combat where they win the fight.
- **Simulacrum and Reverse Polarity wait until damage has actually been dealt.**
- **At full life and out of danger the computer keeps Stream of Life** instead of tapping out for it.
- **The Apprentice now casts Sandstorm** and similar combat-only spells during combat.

## The computer's combat

- **A creature brought back for one turn always attacks.** Shallow Grave is no longer cast after combat or for a creature that cannot attack.
- **Waylay is held for your attack** and cast when the Knights win the combat or save the computer's life.
- **The weaker computer opponents no longer throw their whole table into an all-out attack that two blockers on their trampler would stop.**

## Winter Orb, Storm World, Ivory Tower

- **The computer no longer casts Winter Orb, Smoke, Damping Field or Static Orb when the lock would hold its own side harder than yours.**
- **Under a Winter Orb the computer stops spending its lands on end-of-turn activations** and saves its mana for the better card in its hand.
- **Storm World is cast only when the hands make it hurt you more.**
- **The computer keeps a land it has no use for in hand** while Storm World, The Rack or its own Ivory Tower counts its cards.

## Adventure

- **A place's pane with many choices no longer stands taller than the screen** the first time it is opened with big buttons.
