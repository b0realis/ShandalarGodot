# Shandalar 0.40.12

- The printed timing riders read the turn as it is, not the canonical
  clock: "before attackers are declared", "before blockers", "before the
  combat damage step" mean the NEXT such step of the turn, so an extra
  combat (Relentless Assault) opens them again in the main phase before
  it; "after combat" waits for the turn's last combat. Nettling Imp,
  Siren's Call, Blaze of Glory, Disharmony, Berserk, Rapid Fire, Reset,
  Glyph of Reincarnation, Arcum's Whistle, Norritt, Angus Mackenzie.
- The duel screen no longer stops for an ability its printed timing
  refuses: an untapped Nettling Imp held every priority window of both
  turns and lit the actionable cue on its own turn; now it is a fast
  effect only on an opponent's turn before attackers are declared. The
  AI reads the riders through the same engine query.

No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
