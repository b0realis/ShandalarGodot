# Shandalar 0.40.16

- The registry-wide invariants that only ever ran over the base pool now
  run over every card of every pack as well, from the suite's own
  metadata-only archives, and the sweep found three things to fix:
  - Layer 4 with more than one land-type reader. The base pool has one
    card that reads a land type to decide what it retypes (Conversion),
    and the dependency step (CR 613.8) was two waves — writers, then it.
    Ice Age brings Glaciers and Illusionary Terrain, and a Terrain set to
    "Plains are Forests" reads the very type Conversion writes: it is
    dependent on Conversion whichever entered first, so a Mountain under
    both is a Forest. The engine applied the older one first. Every
    reader now says what it reads and writes, the readers' wave applies
    them in dependency order, and a loop ("Plains are Mountains" against
    Conversion) falls back to timestamps as CR 613.8b says. Nothing
    changes without Ice Age.
  - Sixteen Portal, Portal Second Age and Homelands combat-declaration
    triggers claimed the AI's aftermath forecast, which reads damage and
    death triggers only; the claim was inert and is gone. Baron Sengir
    and Sengir Bats keep theirs, the shape Sengir Vampire has.
  - Three pack Auras the AI would have put on its own creatures are now
    aimed across the table: Merseine, Essence Flare and Phyrexian Boon.
    The other forty-one pack Auras were swept and are friendly on purpose.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
