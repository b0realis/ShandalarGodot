# Shandalar 0.50.16

After Pack 9 the owner asked for one more round over the whole game before
any new card pack: *"Lets make the whole game play smoothly and correctly
and with good AI player."* This release is that round — 84 problems found
across the rules, the duel table, the computer opponent, network play and
saving, and all of them fixed or written down.

- **Rules:**
  - Under the 1997 rules, Time Vault, Basalt Monolith and Mana Vault no
    longer untap for free — the "tapped artifacts stop working" rule had
    switched off their own "doesn't untap" (Time Vault gave an extra turn
    every turn). Zelyon Sword, Spirit Shield and Tawnos's Weaponry give
    their bonus while tapped again.
  - A band stays blocked when the member that was blocked is bounced or
    phased out; the blocker keeps fighting the rest.
  - The 1997 damage-prevention and regeneration steps open on what both
    players can see, so they no longer give away that the opponent holds a
    prevention card; a spell cast before the step no longer resolves
    inside it.
  - Paying for a spell with City of Brass (or a land under Kudzu,
    Manabarbs or Psychic Venom) no longer gets the spell refused: the
    trigger goes on the stack above it.
  - A newer Humility removes menace from Imposing Visage and Goblin War
    Drums; tapping a creature for an ability updates its size at once;
    graveyard triggers are stacked in turn order.
  - Vesuvan Doppelganger's upkeep copy targets; Drain Power lets the
    drained player choose each land's mana ability; Fellwar Stone reads
    Gem Bazaar's chosen colour; Lim-Dûl's Vault lets you dig again;
    Kudzu, Drop of Honey, Juxtapose and Enchantment Alteration rank their
    choices sensibly.
- **Paying mana:** the computer opponent and your double-click auto-cast
  pay with the least mana left over — a Mountain before a Sol Ring, an
  Island before a Mana Vault, a land wearing Psychic Venom or Kudzu only
  when nothing else will do — and stop once Mana Flare, Wild Growth,
  Gauntlet of Might or Overgrowth has covered the cost, so the extra mana
  no longer floats and burns. Fireball under a Mana Flare is offered the
  bigger X.
- **The computer opponent:**
  - It fires burn that is lethal only together, and casts an instant it
    would otherwise discard at cleanup.
  - It no longer harms itself: Fire Imp, Nekrataal, Oubliette or
    Man-o'-War with only its own creatures to target; removal at Blinking
    Spirit or a creature that can still regenerate; a second removal at an
    attacker already answered; Humility, Dread of Night, Light of Day,
    Choke or Cone of Flame into its own board; a creature before its own
    Wrath; drawing itself out of cards; Drop of Honey on its own creature;
    a second Stasis or Pestilence; Time Elemental blocking a 1/1;
    Varchild's War-Riders feeding you Survivors.
  - It uses Fireblast and Spinning Darkness in combat, buys first strike
    for Knight of Stromgald and the Orders, keeps drawing with
    Necropotence, and names a real spell for Demonic Consultation.
  - With Fifth Edition or the Mirage block providing the reprints, it
    plays the Ice Age and Fallen Empires cards (Necropotence, Hecatomb,
    the Blasts) as well as with their own packs on.
  - The Apprentice still makes its mistakes, but never with mana already
    tapped, never on a winning attack or burn, and never the block it
    needs to survive.
  - A board full of licids no longer freezes the table for seconds per
    decision, and a deck of Timetwisters no longer loops.
- **The duel table:**
  - Auto-pass no longer stops at every step because you hold a creature
    with an activated ability.
  - Orcish Catapult, Cuombajj Witches and seven other cards that asked you
    for a target the opponent or chance chooses can be used again.
  - A spell lights up only when it has something to aim at and its costs
    can be paid; it is refused before any land is tapped.
  - A tutor can fail to find; a spell finished while the opponent holds
    priority waits for yours; "any player may activate" abilities on the
    opponent's cards work; Abandon Hope's X is capped by your hand.
  - When nothing of yours can block, the empty block is declared for you
    (a Stop on the blockers icon keeps the old stop).
- **SGManalink:** protocol **29**; LAN peers need matching 0.50.16 builds.
  Choices name which of two same-named cards they mean, the network table
  stops for a payable Sabertooth Cobra ransom, unpayable special actions
  are no longer offered, and a tournament's return to the hall survives a
  dropped connection. Holding Firestorm no longer stalls the host.
- **The agent's seat (MCP):** a brief disconnect at a LAN table no longer
  concedes the agent's game; the decision-menu helper never concedes on
  its own; `referee_stop` on a kept game is not lost; `--table NAME` joins
  that table; an unseeded duel reports its seed only in the result; `until`
  stops in the 1997 damage steps; Magnetic Web, Circling Vultures and
  shadow reach the agent.
- **Decks and saving:**
  - Closing the window in the Deck Builder, from the Booster Draft setup,
    or during a draft started from the builder, now asks to save unsaved
    decks first.
  - A deck no longer demands a card pack after its last card from that
    pack is taken out (a deck saved that way before this release asks for
    the pack until it is opened and saved once).
  - Deck titles in other alphabets or with accented letters no longer
    share a save file.
  - A decks folder kept as a link (a synced folder) counts as your own.
  - The Booster Draft setup responds at once, and its count arrows work.

Known and listed in the record: Illusionary Mask does not check the colours
of the mana spent on X.
