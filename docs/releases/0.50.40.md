# Shandalar 0.50.40

Fixes on top of 0.50.34, the published release.

- **Choose the Situation Bar's frame.** Options has a new **Situation
  Bar frame** list, with a piece of the bar under it wearing your
  choice. **ShandalarGodot** is the frame the bar has worn until now:
  pale along the top and left, slate along the bottom and right.
  **Classic** is the 1997 bar's ridge, a pale line all the way round
  with a dark one beside it, in pale blue-grey and dark ink; **Classic
  pale** is the same ridge in the light and dark of the bar's own
  stone; **Classic thin** is Classic pale with lines half as wide. The
  combat window's and the duel log's title bars wear the same frame.
  It holds from the next duel.

## From 0.50.39

- **Test hands.** Magic Battle has a new **Test hands** button, at the
  bottom of its settings under the ante and match settings. Choose
  the decks and the opponent as usual, then set up each side: the cards in
  hand (picked from the deck, dealt at random, or any card from your
  enabled packs), the cards on the table (tapped or untapped, and a
  creature summoning sick or ready), the graveyard, the top of the library
  and the life total; then whose turn it is and the step it starts in.
  **Go test!** drops you straight into that turn: no coin toss, no
  mulligan. **Restart test** plays the same test again with the same
  shuffle, **Edit test** takes you back to change it, and tests can be
  saved by name. A test is never played for ante and nothing about it is
  recorded.
- **The computer plays combos.** From Magician up, the computer knows the
  classic loops of the card pool: Sliver Queen with Heartstone and
  Ashnod's Altar, Aluren with Man-o'-War and Soul Warden or Pandemonium,
  Enduring Renewal with Ornithopter and Ashnod's Altar or Goblin
  Bombardment, Basalt Monolith with Power Artifact, and Earthcraft with
  the Queen. It puts the pieces together and runs a loop for a reason
  (lethal damage, the mana for a lethal Fireball, a swarm, or life) and
  stops when that is done. At Sorcerer and Wizard it also finds loops of
  its own among the cards it can see. It aims its removal and
  counterspells at the pieces of your loop.
- **No loop can freeze a duel.** A loop that would never end is stopped
  at 1000 steps — spells, abilities and triggers in one step — with a
  message: "You created an infinite loop — stopping at 1000 steps." (or
  the computer's name, when the loop was its). Whatever is already on the
  Spell Chain resolves, and the duel goes on with the next step.
- **Red messages are easier to read.** A refused action or a stopped
  loop is written on the Situation Bar in red with a white outline, so
  it stands out from the bar instead of sinking into it.
- **The computer's turn always ends.** With Aluren on the table, a
  computer holding a Shrieking Drake or a Man-o'-War could cast it for
  free, have it return itself to its hand and cast it again, for ever:
  its turn never ended. It now never repeats a position it has already
  played from, and outside a combo it means to run it never casts one
  card more than eight times in a turn.
- **Creatures that enter with counters are sized right.** The computer
  now judges a Workhorse, a Spike or a Triskelion by the size it will
  have on the table, not by the 0/0 or 1/1 printed on the card, so it
  plays, keeps and brings these creatures back the way their size
  deserves; a Corpse Dance now raises a Workhorse to attack.

## From 0.50.38
- **Your own card back.** Options has a new **Card back** section:
  **Choose...** puts a picture of yours on the back of every card. Use a
  PNG, JPG or WEBP, upright; **456 × 646 pixels** is best, the shape of
  a card. A picture of another shape is trimmed from the middle to fit,
  and a phone photo is turned the way it was held. **Remove** brings the
  usual back again.

## From 0.50.37

- **Cards can be moved right to the edge of the table.** A card you drag
  around the battlefield used to stop short of the edges: a little short
  at the top and sides, and well short of the bottom. Now it goes all the
  way, so its edge touches the edge of the table, on your side and on
  your opponent's. A tapped card parked against an edge stays whole
  while it is turned, and goes back to exactly where you put it when it
  untaps.

## From 0.50.36

- **LAN hosting is calmer under attack.** A LAN connection that sends
  more than 4 KB of junk before joining is let go straight away, even when
  the host's computer is busy. Before, a busy host could print an error
  line every frame for a second and a half before letting it go.

## From 0.50.35

- **The life panel keeps its picture's shape.** The picture behind your
  life total, and the duelist's face the panel turns over to, were
  stretched to fill the panel, which is wider than the 1997 one, so the
  skull, the crystal ball and the green leaf frame came out nearly twice
  as wide as drawn. Now the picture meets the panel halfway: it is
  widened only a little and loses only a little of its top and bottom,
  so it fills the panel and keeps its look.
