# Shandalar 0.60.15

- **No freeze with card packs.** Setting out or resuming an adventure with
  its card packs switched off in Options no longer freezes the window: the
  hourglass shows at once while the packs are switched on behind it. The
  first town, trade, reward or lair of a session with card packs no longer
  stalls: their prices are worked out in the background as the world opens.
- **The card trader's name box** no longer stutters with many card packs:
  the list follows a moment after you stop typing, and long lists fill
  without a freeze.
- **Snow-Covered basics** from Ice Age are basic lands on Shandalar: a
  shop's land place may hold one, four come for one amulet at a trader, and
  a small deck may win one in place of a basic land.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
