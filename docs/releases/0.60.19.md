# Shandalar 0.60.19

Pack 10, the Urza block — Urza's Saga, Urza's Legacy and Urza's Destiny —
joins the game, its duels, its deck builder and its adventure. And an
adventure can now travel from one device to another.

- **Pack 10 — The Urza Block.** Urza's Saga, Urza's Legacy and Urza's
  Destiny as one optional pack: 636 printings, 621 names, **591 new
  cards**, all with their rules — none is left uncastable. The 30 reprints
  use the cards the game already has (18 of them come with Pack 10 when
  their own pack is off). Build it with `tools/pack_10_urza_block.py`; turn
  it on in Options → Card Packs. Its badge is 10-USG; the Deck Builder's
  Extras get gears, a hammer and a flask, one for each set. Needs 0.60.19.
  With all ten packs the game has 3,684 different cards.
- **New to play:** **echo** — pay the cost again at your next upkeep or
  sacrifice the card; **cycling** — click a card in your hand and choose
  Cycling to discard it for a new card; the **Opal, Veiled and Hidden**
  enchantments that turn into creatures, and Opalescence, which meets
  Humility the way the rules say; spells that **untap the lands that paid
  for them** (Frantic Search, Snap, Peregrine Drake, Great Whale,
  Palinchron), the Monoliths and Voltaic Key, Tolarian Academy, Gaea's
  Cradle and Serra's Sanctum; **Yawgmoth's Will** — play lands and spells
  from your graveyard, straight from the graveyard view; Temporal
  Aperture — click your library to play its top card; Show and Tell,
  Replenish and Exhume putting their cards onto the battlefield all at
  once; the verse enchantments; Defensive Formation, Okk and Outmaneuver
  in combat; Metalworker, Thran Turbine, Contamination, Storage Matrix and
  Damping Engine.
- **The computer opponent** plays the new cards: echo paid when the card
  is worth it and let go when it isn't, cycling the cards it can't use,
  untap spells cast when the lands they hand back pay for the next spell,
  Yawgmoth's Will only with a graveyard worth replaying, Show and Tell and
  the gifts (Donate, Gilded Drake, Sleeper Agent) weighed for both sides,
  the verse enchantments cashed when they clear the board, Serra's Hymn
  kept for damage it can see coming, spare mana spent on Temporal
  Aperture, and Sneak Attack only for an attack that wins or nearly does.
- **Help** explains the block in plain words on nine new "Abilities —
  Urza block" pages: echo and cycling, the enchantments that become
  creatures, untapping and mana, counters to cash in, cards put onto the
  battlefield, other zones and revealed cards, graveyards and
  counterspells, combat, and the notable cards.
- **In the adventure:** tick Pack 10 on Embark, alone or with Packs 2–9,
  to add its cards to the shops, trades, rewards and lairs. Its strongest
  cards — Tolarian Academy, Yawgmoth's Will, Yawgmoth's Bargain, Tinker,
  Windfall, Memory Jar, Time Spiral, Gaea's Cradle, Serra's Sanctum,
  Voltaic Key, Fluctuator, Grim Monolith and Frantic Search — are never
  sold in shops, and cost four amulets at a trader.
- **Take an adventure to another device.** Save / Load has **Export** and
  **Import**. Export saves the highlighted adventure, every save of it, as
  one file wherever you choose; on a phone or a headset, choose Downloads.
  Import brings one in on any computer, phone, headset or browser, checks
  it all first, and asks before it replaces an adventure that's already
  there. On Android and the Meta Quest your saves now live in the game's
  folder on the shared storage, `Android/data/<package>/files/adventures`,
  where `adb pull` can copy them; saves you already have move there by
  themselves. The Quest's clean-install script offers to copy your saved
  adventures to the computer before it deletes anything.
- **Fixes you may notice:** Psychic Purge now punishes an opponent's Wheel
  of Fortune that discards it. Gloom no longer taxes, and Cursed Totem and
  Null Rod no longer stop, cards in your hand — Elvish Spirit Guide works
  under Cursed Totem. Living Death returns every creature at the same
  moment, so a returned Soul Warden sees all the others arrive. Island
  Sanctuary, Fasting and Breathstealer's Crypt ask you in your draw step
  instead of deciding for you, and Mana Flare asks which type of mana to
  add when your land made more than one. Ali from Cairo (and Worship)
  limit the life you lose, not the damage dealt, so Hypnotic Specter and
  Spirit Link still see it. Kismet taps an enchantment that enters as a
  creature under Opalescence, and Ashnod's Transmogrant's artifact type
  stays. As the defender dividing a blocked trampler's damage (defensive
  banding, Defensive Formation), you put its extra damage on your own
  life counter. The computer opponent no longer casts Hurkyl's Recall at a
  player who owns no artifacts.
- **Casting reads as in 1997:** while a spell waits for its mana, the bar
  says what to tap — "CASTING: Timetwister, Tap {2}{U}" — and counts the
  mana down as you tap (it used to say "Tap Timetwister").
- **Adventure plugins:** a new land for the adventure is now a small package
  of data — JSON, text, pictures and music, never code — that you drop into
  the `expanded_adventure` folder beside the card packs and tick in Embark's
  new **Expanded adventure plugins...** window, under the card packs. Each
  plugin names the card packs it needs (ticking it ticks them); the packs
  behave as they always do. The game comes with one, **The Last Refuge**: sail
  to an island of Sarpadian refugees, win five communities' pledges and
  challenge the Ebon Crown — a finished game-design and learning demo of
  original fiction for Pack 2: Fallen Empires. If a plugin goes missing or
  changes, your save still opens: the land waits as you left it and its ship
  still takes you home. Authors get a local guide
  (`expanded_adventure/GUIDE/index.html`) and a tool,
  `tools/adventure_plugin.py`, to start, check, package and compare plugins;
  new lands are the community's to make. Options shows the plugins folder,
  and Help has a page on them.
- **The right color, without asking:** while a spell waits for its mana,
  clicking a dual land, Birds of Paradise or City of Brass taps it for the
  color the cost still needs; you're asked only when the choice is really
  yours. Fellwar Stone and Reflecting Pool make the color the cost still
  needs too, when you click them and when the double-click pays; one you
  marked **Don't auto tap this card** asks for its color, as in 1997.
- **See your Don't auto tap marks:** a new button under the large card
  shows which of your cards you marked Don't auto tap this card (right-click
  a card on your table and choose it). Each marked card wears a tap arrow
  struck through in red at its right edge, and a marked land stands on its
  own beside its pile. The button remembers its setting.
- **Life at 0 in an adventure:** the Campaign Settings have a new row.
  Tick **Negative life is survivable** and every duel of that adventure
  plays the 1997 rule: a duelist at 0 life or less loses only when the
  phase ends, and lives on by gaining life back before then. So the life
  you gain from the blow that ends a duel, such as Spirit Link's, counts,
  and a dungeon carries it to the next room. Unticked, the Rules in Options
  decide, as before; As 1997 ticks it.
- **A text change shows in the card's words:** a land under Magical Hack, a
  creature under Sleight of Mind and the like show their new text on the big
  card, the changed words in italics, and in the tooltip above the printed
  rules.
- **Riddle cards you can read:** the dungeon scroll's riddle card is shown
  at full big-card size, the creature riddle's card is larger, and a left
  click on either opens the card reader.
- **MPN has a home of its own:** Magic Public Notation, the open format
  the game's Export MPN writes, now lives at
  github.com/b0realis/MagicPublicNotation-MPN — the specification, an
  implementation guide and examples — for other Magic programs to read,
  implement and help develop.
- **Cursors at their normal size, in your color:** the duel's targeting
  crosshair is back to its 1997 size, and the Hi-res cursors switch is gone
  — every pointer has one, normal size. Options → Cursor color dresses the
  adventure's pointers (the arrow, the hand, the walker, the sword and the
  places) in Gold, Electric blue, Green, Red, Orange, Purple, White or
  Black.
- **Booster Draft's building time stands out** in gold, so it isn't missed.
- **The Deck Builder's deck scrolls smoothly:** the wheel, the bar and the
  keys glide the deck by the pixel, a row cut at the edge shows there is
  more, and purple arrows at its right say when cards are hidden above or
  below — a click on one moves a row.
- **Magic Battle's dice:** a die beside each seat's heading rolls that
  seat a deck, a name and a look at random; your seat's die also sets your
  territory background to the deck's color.
- **See the card behind the name:** in a window that lists cards by name —
  Bazaar of Baghdad's discard, a library search, any card to pick — the
  large card shows the card under your pointer, and so do the Sideboard
  window between duels and Test hands' card picker.
- **LAN games:** Pack 10 plays at a LAN table too. Cycling from your
  hand, a land from your graveyard under Yawgmoth's Will and Temporal
  Aperture's top card are played from the networked screen, and every new
  question reaches the guest's own screen. The LAN protocol is now 31, so
  every machine needs 0.60.19 and the same card packs.
- **Test hands:** a permanent on the table can be marked **Echo due**, so
  the test duel asks for its echo at the next upkeep.
- **More fixes:** the computer opponent answers a lethal spell with its
  counterspell first (or turns it back on you with Deflection when that
  wins), judges a divided spell by the damage each target
  really takes, casts Relentless Assault after it has attacked, and no
  longer wastes Vodalian War Machine's pump, Tourach's Gate, Melee,
  Phelddagrif's gifts or Aether Storm's life. Effects that take abilities
  away now follow today's rules: Kormus Bell under Titania's Song still
  makes the Swamps 1/1 black creatures, and Sleight of Mind changes only a
  color word the card itself prints. At a
  private hotseat you act only for your own seat, a Clone question never
  names a face-down creature (your own you may look at), and the X
  question and library search close when the duel ends. The duel log
  stays above the bar at a large UI scale. At LAN tables a guest whose seat
  ended, or whose host closed, is told why and can leave, Escape and a
  pad's B close the lobby's windows, and long deck names fit. A deck's
  notes keep their first line's indent, and Illusionary Mask hides no ante
  card in a duel not played for ante.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
