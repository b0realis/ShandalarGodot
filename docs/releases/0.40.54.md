# Shandalar 0.40.54

The owner, after the title learned to stand before the pool: *"Do you
suggest any other low hanging fruit vr optimizations we can do?"* — of
the five on the table, *"Yes"* to the one that was a hole 0.40.53 had
opened, and *"Low processor mode should also be toggable in options"*
— which it already is, and this note says where.

- **The press that comes before the pool waits on the title.** With
  the card pool compiling on its thread the title stands about 0.8 s
  after the process starts on the desk and the pool reports some 3.5 s
  later — on a handheld, longer. A press on Magic Battle, Gauntlet or
  Deck Builder inside that gap used to reach the pool from the next
  screen's `_ready`, which joins the thread: the title froze on the
  press for the rest of the build. The dead click had moved, not gone.
  Now the three screens that read a card are held on the title until
  the frame the pool is in; the pressed button says `Loading cards…`
  in place of its label meanwhile (the version corner already did), a
  second card screen pressed during the wait takes it over, and the
  held screen opens the frame the thread reports. Options and Help read
  no card and open at once; Exit quits at once. Measured under Xvfb
  with a press 30 frames after the title stood: the press returned in
  0 ms, the title kept rendering (frame 943 at 4.3 s), the setup
  screen stood 317 ms after the pool's report — its own build, the
  same as a press made after it.
- **Power saver is already in Options → Display.** The engine's
  low-processor mode — the screen redrawn only when something changes,
  the process resting between frames — is the `Power saver` toggle,
  on by default on every handheld (the Quest 3 counts as one) and off
  on a desktop; it takes effect at once, no restart. Nothing changed
  here; it is named so it can be found.
- **What waits on the headset's report.** Pack discovery onto the
  thread (1.0 s of the 0.40.52 start) and the pool compile itself are
  decided on the 0.40.53/0.40.54 start report's `card pool: N cards in
  M ms (background)` line, not before: the desk says 3.4 s beside the
  rendering title; the headset's own number is what the next step is
  measured against.
- **What the tests say.** `tests/unit/test_card_registry_background_2026_09_30.gd`
  gains two (10 tests): a press under the build returns in under
  100 ms with the button reading `Loading cards…`, nothing opens for a
  frame, a second card screen takes the wait over and restores the
  first button, and the held screen is the current scene two frames
  after the thread is in; Help opens at once under the same build.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
