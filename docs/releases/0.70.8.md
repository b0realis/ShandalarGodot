# Shandalar 0.70.8

- **The Duelist's Ring is round on the map too.** Its place on the map is
  now the round stone ring its window shows: a low wall with a gap at the
  front, posts and rope round it, and the two banners behind.
- **A thousand opening hands, counted.** The Deck Builder's Stats window
  has a new button on its Hand page, **1000 hands**: it deals a thousand
  opening hands of seven from your deck and shows what they held — how
  many had no land, one, two and so on, and how many you would keep;
  which of your lands came, and how many of each; how often the hand
  could make each color your spells ask for, and all of them at once;
  how often there was a play on turn one or by turn two, a spell the
  hand's own lands pay for, a creature; and the spells seen most. Press
  it again for another thousand; **New hand** brings the cards back.
- **Bug fixes.**

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
