# Shandalar 0.70.15

The computer plays more of its cards well, and three groups of cards now work as printed.

## Cards that now work as printed

- **Library of Lat-Nam, Misfortune and Fatal Lore:** your opponent chooses the outcome as the spell is cast, so both players can respond knowing it.
- **Matopi Golem, Skeleton Scavengers and a Debt of Loyalty creature:** with several regeneration shields on it, you choose which one is used when a spell or ability would destroy it.
- **Opal Titan** gets protection from the colors a countered creature spell last had.

## The computer's spells

- **Silhouette is kept to save the computer's own creature** from a spell or ability aimed at it, and no longer thrown at yours.
- **Reincarnation is kept for the computer's own creature that is about to die**, in combat or to removal.
- **Transmutation and About Face are held for a combat where the switch decides the fight**, or used to kill a creature with no power.
- **Indestructible Aura saves a creature from burn or a losing block**, and Anoint is no longer cast with nothing to prevent.

## The computer's one-turn creatures

- **Corpse Dance is no longer cast into a blocker that stops its creature cold**, and is cast during your attack when the creature's block is worth it, with buyback when the computer can pay.
- **The computer uses Zirilan of the Claw:** it calls a Dragon before its attack, or as a blocker when you attack.
- **Balduvian Dead, Hornet Cannon and Echo Chamber** make their one-turn creature only when it can attack to some effect; the first two also make it as a surprise blocker.

## Locks and taxes

- **The computer no longer casts Marble Titan, Storage Matrix, Sands of Time, Mana Breach or Mana Vortex when they would hurt it more than you.**
- **Under Storage Matrix it untaps the kind it needs most.** Under Sands of Time it taps idle lands before its turn where that is free. Under Mana Breach it keeps cheap spells that are not worth a land. It never casts into a Spellshock that would kill it.
- **Under Winter Orb the computer saves its lands for the better card in hand** instead of an ability or an X spell.
- **The Apprentice and the Magician keep playing cards out over a Black Vise** and hold small spells under The Rack.
