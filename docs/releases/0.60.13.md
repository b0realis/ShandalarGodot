# Shandalar 0.60.13

Fixes on top of 0.60.12.

- **Campaign card packs on load.** A saved adventure whose card pack is
  switched off enables it again and says so on arrival; one whose pack is
  missing names it in the save list instead of loading without its cards.
  Cards the game cannot load no longer enter duels or stakes.
- **Tasks.** Follow several tasks at once, an arrow for each. Arzakon's
  portal has its own violet arrow, drawn over the others, with a switch in
  Tasks to hide it. Dungeons in Tasks show their names, a chosen task is no
  longer lost after loading, and arrow names no longer cover their arrows.
- **The journal** reads "Day N: …" again in an ordinary campaign, older
  saves' lines included. The wise man's news is a dated journal entry
  instead of a permanent Known clue.
- **One life and the ending.** A One-life adventure whose Final Battle was
  lost now ends on loading any of its saves, and a won adventure always asks
  End or Return to Shandalar again.
- **Days on the road.** The save list, the final record and the Hall of Fame
  count every day of the journey.
- **Shops.** A shop place whose card pack is unavailable names the pack and
  restocks with another card.
- **LAN.** A computer opponent at a LAN table gets the same reading pause as
  at home: its new spell and target are named on the bar and shown in the
  Showcase.
- **Help** describes Card packs, Randomize, the four hero tabs (T for
  Tasks), the Quickslot (Ctrl+S) and the card rules for pack cards.
- **Tools.** Deck Lab, AutoDeck and the lab query run from a checkout
  without the script errors they printed since 0.60.6, and a connected
  gamepad (every Steam Deck) no longer breaks the MCP tools' answers.

Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain usable. For LAN games,
use matching game versions and card packs on every machine.
