# Shandalar 0.50.33

A fix from playtesting, on top of everything in 0.50.32 and the versions
before it back to 0.50.20 (none released on its own), which follow below.

- **The stone windows and buttons are sharp.** The sandstone behind
  Magic Battle, Options, Help and the other stone windows, and on the
  main menu's buttons, was one 400x350 picture stretched to fill each
  window and squeezed into each button, which blurred it on a large
  screen. It is now laid at its own size, so the stone keeps its fine
  grain at any window size, with either skin. The game makes the
  picture into a tile that joins without a visible seam and evens out
  its light, so the stone looks the same from one side to the other.

## From 0.50.32

- **Your turn no longer stops in its upkeep for nothing.** After the
  computer's turn the duel waited at the start of yours, in the upkeep,
  even with no red dot there. It now goes on by itself unless you have a
  Stop on that phase, something must be decided, or you can answer what
  is happening.

## From 0.50.31

- **A hi-res skin.** `python3 tools/upscale_skin.py --skin
  original_skin.zip` makes a second skin out of your 1997 one,
  `original_skin_realesrgan-anime_2x.zip`: the same files under the same
  names, every picture at twice the size with Real-ESRGAN's anime model,
  a free and open-source upscaler, keeping the 1997 colours, and the
  music, sounds, fonts and coin toss as they were. It runs on Linux with
  Pillow and a Vulkan graphics driver, and fetches the upscaler once.
  Choose it in Options → Skin or drop it on the window, and it is worn
  from the next start — the same layout with twice the pixels, so the
  panels, frames, icons and the mana stripes on the cards stay sharp on a
  large or 4K screen. Choose the 1997 skin again to go back; there is no
  switch.
- **Mix and match.** Put a 1997 picture back into the hi-res zip under
  its own name and that place wears it. A skin may also carry a
  `catalogue.txt` saying which of its files the game wears in each place,
  and a picture may come at its 1997 size or at two, three or four times
  it; `skin/SKIN.txt` explains both for anyone drawing a skin, and
  `python3 tools/skin_catalogue.py --check my_skin.zip` checks one.
- **One skin at a time.** The skin zip you choose dresses the game
  alone: the one beside the game no longer fills in what yours lacks,
  and a loose skin folder is read only when no skin zip is worn (or with
  **Use the skin folder instead of the zip**).

## From 0.50.27

- **See what your copy gives before you import it.**
  `python3 tools/mtg_assets.py --check /path/to/game` lists every file the
  game can wear, where each would come from and what is missing, and
  writes nothing. A complete 1997 install gives every one of them.
- **No 1997 copy? A Manalink install as a backup.** Add `--manalink` and
  the tools take a later Manalink install's own files where they fit the
  shape the game draws; some are Manalink's restyles of the 1997 art, and
  a few stay the game's own drawing. The original 1997 game stays the
  default and the only source of the skin the releases carry; a skin made
  this way is for your own copy.
- **The skin catalogue as a table.** `skin/SKIN.csv`, beside
  `skin/SKIN.txt` in every package, lists every file of the skin zip by
  name, with its size and format, whether the game reads it and the 1997
  file it comes from: a checklist for anyone drawing a skin of their own.

## From 0.50.26

- **One text face on every computer.** Rules text, the duel log and every
  window (Stats, the Gauntlet, Options and the rest) use the game's own
  face, Spectral, set a little tighter, whether or not the original skin
  is installed. Before, the face depended on the skin, so the same window
  could look different from one computer to the next. Every font the game
  ships is an open-source font under the SIL Open Font License. The empty
  cells of the Deck Builder's Stats grid now show their dot.
- **The damage dagger** on a damaged creature is the game's own on every
  table, with or without the skin.
- **The original skin requires only the original 1997 game.**
  `tools/mtg_assets.py` and `tools/import_original.py` build it from your
  copy of the original 1997 MicroProse game (the CD or its install folder)
  and nothing else; later re-releases and fan conversions are no longer
  read. A skin you made before still works: the game ignores the text face
  and dagger in it, and a skin zip made from an older import leaves the
  old text face out.
- **Full-screen card on click** (Options → Display) is on by default, as
  it already was on handhelds. Untick it there to turn it off.

## From 0.50.22

Fixes from a check of the whole game and its documents.

- **LAN tables:**
  - A question for you now closes the duel's mini help and comes to the
    front, and a click meant for the help's Close no longer answers it.
  - The Pause window stays on top: a question that arrives while you are
    paused waits behind it, and a click on the Pause window never answers
    it.
  - Escape opens the Pause window again; over the mini help it only closes
    the help.
  - With the tournament Master Panel open over your own duel, keys no
    longer act on the hidden table; Escape just closes the panel.
  - The Game Browser names the button that clears a selected host
    ("Stop LAN search"), and a selection the lobby had dropped can no longer
    refuse a pasted invitation.
- **Options:** a rule you set on a new profile is no longer changed at the
  next start.
- **Help:** the targeting cursor names the key you bound to Cancel, and the
  card packs are called by their names on the Card Packs screen.
- **Packages:** each is about 4 MB smaller — pictures only the project's
  own page uses are no longer packed into the game. The setup notes in every
  package give the right commands for the art tools (they are in `tools/`),
  and the handheld notes put card packs in `cardpacks/`.
- **For people who build the game:** a Quest build without the editor's
  settings or the Android SDK no longer stops silently, `shandalar.sh
  --help` works from any folder, Ctrl-C stops a duel soak, and the Quest
  clean-install script reports an empty log correctly.

## From 0.50.21

Three additions — who decides the order of two effects on the same
damage, forced sacrifices that can wait, and help inside the duel — on top
of everything in 0.50.20, which was not released on its own. 0.50.20 was a
robustness and bug-fix round, measured and tested across the rules, the
cards, the computer opponent, the screens, saved files, the network and the
handhelds: 105 problems were found and fixed or written down; the record is
`docs/robustness-campaign-2026-10-12.md`. Its changes follow the three
additions below.

- **When two effects change the same damage, who decides their order.**
  A Lightning Bolt aimed at you with your Forethought Amulet and an
  opponent's Furnace of Rath out: the Amulet first makes it 2 and the
  Furnace doubles that to 4; the Furnace first makes it 6 and the Amulet
  brings it down to 2.
  - Under the modern rules, when a spell or ability would deal such damage
    to you or your creature, you choose which effect applies first: "Which
    applies first to the 3 damage from Lightning Bolt?" The computer
    opponent works out the order that hurts it least.
  - **Options › Rules › Fixed order for damage effects** keeps the 1997
    game's way: nobody is asked, and the effects apply in a fixed order. The
    1997 rules preset turns it on; the modern presets leave it off.
  - The option travels with a LAN table's rules, and a settings file saved
    under a preset by an older version keeps that preset.
- **Forced sacrifices can wait.** A new switch, **Options › Rules › Forced
  sacrifices wait on the chain**, covers every card that must go when a
  condition is met: a Pirate Ship when you control no Islands, Gorilla Pack
  with no Forests, Goblins of the Flarg when you control a Dwarf, Jihad,
  Floodgate and the rest.
  - Off — under every preset — they go at once, as in the 1997 game.
  - On, the sacrifice waits on the Spell Chain like any other triggered
    ability, so the card gets one more use: a Pirate Ship can ping once more
    as your last Island goes. The computer opponent uses its own card that
    way too.
- **Help inside the duel.** "Help..." in the duel's right-click menus now
  opens a small help panel over the table for exactly what you clicked —
  a card, your library, the mana pool, the Showcase, the Phase Bar, the
  combat window, the Spell Chain, the graveyard. "Help for this card..." and
  "Help for this phase..." work too. The duel waits while it is open; the
  full Help is still on the main menu. The graveyard and the Spell Chain
  now have their right-click menus.

From 0.50.20:

- **Smoother duels:**
  - One computer action no longer rebuilds the whole table once per
    change: a big X spell tapping many lands used to freeze the screen for
    a second and a half and hold hundreds of megabytes. The screen now
    redraws once per frame.
  - Card pack pictures stay within the same memory bound as the skin's
    card art, so browsing the Deck Builder with packs on no longer grows
    without end.
- **Rules:**
  - Damage takes into account effects that apply only after another
    effect changed it: Ali from Cairo at 4 life with Furnace of Rath out
    now ends at 1 life after a Lightning Bolt.
  - Circle of Protection and the other "source of your choice" cards can
    name a source that has already left play, such as a sacrificed Mogg
    Fanatic, and such a source keeps the colour and controller it last had.
  - A Wrath or a Nevinyrral's Disk judges every creature on the board as it
    stood before any of them left (Humility with a Rukh Egg, Volrath's
    Shapeshifter mid-Wrath).
  - A creature that stops being a creature leaves combat.
  - Withering Wisps' activation limit and Fyndhorn Druid's "if it was
    blocked" now work; Venarian Gold, Living Artifact, Cocoon and Tetravus
    remove their counters cleanly.
  - A trigger with only one legal target, and a trampler that cannot get
    past its one blocker, no longer stop the duel to ask.
- **The computer opponent:**
  - It no longer holds back its creatures for a counterspell it cannot pay,
    and stops holding counter mana when the board is killing it.
  - Dark Ritual only when it leads to a spell; no life gain at full life;
    paid first strike is respected when blocking; a Karoo's two mana are
    spent where they fit.
  - Dream Halls and Aluren are held when they would help the opponent; big
    licid boards are decided about twice as fast.
  - It now uses Gangrenous Zombies, Time Bomb, Goblin Lyre, Spoils of
    Evil, Forgotten Lore and Gorilla War Cry.
- **The duel screen:**
  - At a private hotseat, Concede from the right-click menu gives up the
    duel of the player at the screen.
  - Number questions answer with the number you press (Land Tax's "up to
    3": press 3, get three).
  - The pause key opens the pause menu under a question, over the
    full-size card and during the coin toss.
  - Spells and abilities with no legal target are greyed out instead of
    opening a target prompt you can only cancel.
  - Notes on the Situation Bar stay readable; a screenshot or log that
    could not be saved says so.
  - The full-size card works with the controller pointer, closes on a
    right click, and switches its text box on Enter only.
- **Menus and controllers:**
  - Popups keep the keyboard and controller focus and hand it back when
    they close; Escape and the back button work on Help, Options, Card
    Packs and Magic Battle.
  - The Controls page can be left with the controller pointer on; a
    controller unplugged mid-press is let go.
  - On ArkOS, Select now opens the menu even after typing in the Deck
    Builder's search box.
  - Card Packs shows Pack 6's Second Age.
- **Decks:**
  - Random decks in Magic Battle respect the chosen format.
  - Save after Load updates the deck you opened.
  - Import reads MTG Arena, Moxfield/MTGO and Forge lists.
  - Decks with long titles save.
  - A Booster Draft shows every card it dealt, and its Card pool lists
    whole sets, Fifth Edition and reprints included.
  - The Gauntlet's music stops under its duels.
- **Files and settings:**
  - A skin zip the game won't wear is no longer deleted at start; Options ›
    Skin says which and why.
  - A settings value of the wrong kind is read as meant or as its default;
    a settings file that could not be read or saved is said on the title
    screen and in Options.
  - Saves follow linked files and leave read-only ones alone; two copies of
    the game on one profile no longer undo each other.
  - A damaged copy of a card pack can no longer pass as checked.
- **LAN and the agent tools:**
  - A host that closes tells its guests "The host closed its tables."
    instead of leaving them reconnecting forever.
  - Hostile connections are cut off quickly; network updates cost a half
    to a quarter of what they did.
  - The MCP server survives malformed input and reports a crashed game;
    invitation secrets stay off the command line.
- **Handhelds:**
  - The Quest scripts never leave half a zip on the headset, say when it is
    out of space, ignore a second adb device and name misnamed packs.
  - The ArkOS launcher says why it stopped.
  - The timing log stays out of the tools' output, measures nested work
    honestly and can be switched on on the Quest.
  - A desktop where V-Sync is refused is capped at the screen's rate.
