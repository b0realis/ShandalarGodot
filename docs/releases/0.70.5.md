# Shandalar 0.70.5

- **Smoother duels.** The table no longer rebuilds every card each time
  something happens: a card that did not change stays as it is, so the
  card under the pointer keeps its glow and its tooltip. The pictures of
  both decks are read while the duel is dealt, not as each card first
  shows. The computer plays at an even beat: the time it spent thinking
  is taken off its pause, and a long thought no longer freezes the frame
  twice. The large card is not drawn again for the card it already shows.
- **A smoother adventure map.** Walking no longer stutters at a tile's
  edge on slow machines: the world, the small map and the big map redraw
  only what changed. The game is saved in the background as you meet a
  creature, enter a lair or leave a place, so the map does not freeze for
  a moment there. A plugin's pictures and an island's music are read ahead
  as well, and a line spoken in a dungeon no longer slows the board.
- **Rules fixes.** A creature that taps to attack can no longer pay its
  own attack tax, such as Propaganda's; a creature that stays home, or
  one with vigilance, still can. Reincarnation on a Clone brings it back
  as a fresh copy, and a creature that dies together with the enchanted
  one can be raised. Two players' Jade Monoliths on one creature each
  send their own share of damage to their own controller. A once-a-turn
  ability no longer loses or gains a use when another ability of the card
  ends mid-turn.
- **The computer wastes less.** It no longer pays life for mana, or taps
  for a prevention or an ability, when the play then cannot be made.
- **Selection windows.** The arrow and number keys no longer answer a
  question lying under another window. After a click, a later Space does
  not answer the clicked line. In a tutor's list, press the select key or
  click the selected card once more to let it go, so you can still choose
  to find nothing; OK counts once.
- **Closing the window after a duel.** If you close the game's window
  when an adventure duel is over, its result is kept: a duel you lost,
  and the card lost in it, no longer comes back on the next load. A duel
  still being played saves nothing, as before.
- **Adventure fixes.** A card picked from a list is given once, never
  twice on a double click. A town you just chose in no longer vanishes if
  you open the map in the same instant. Closing the window from the
  adventure's deck builder asks about extra copies, as Done does. A deck
  larger than the deck builder can show keeps all its cards.
- **Saves.** Damaged saves are read more forgivingly: a broken quest, a
  short list of places or an odd value in a plugin's progress no longer
  stops the save from opening. An adventure whose save file you renamed
  can be exported, and an old renamed save becomes a slot of its own
  adventure.
- **Places plugins.** A dungeon raised by a place stays on your map and in
  your Tasks after it moves. A place added to an adventure already under
  way never lands under your hero, on Arzakon's portal or on a lair, and
  it appears on the maps only once you have seen its land. For authors,
  the authoring tool's compatibility check now warns when a free service
  gains prices or a price list is shortened, its edits are all-or-nothing,
  and a place on the mainland and the islands is played through on both.
- **LAN.** When a guest closes the game, the host learns of it at once
  and the opponent no longer waits five minutes. You can concede while the
  other player is away. Join in the Game Browser takes the table you
  clicked when several tables share a name.
- **Portable game.** Asking to make the game portable and then canceling
  no longer leaves an empty portable folder behind, which made the next
  start look as if your settings, decks and adventures were gone. The copy
  is made beside it and becomes the portable folder only when it is
  whole. Escape cancels the portable window and stays in Options.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
