# Shandalar 0.60.2

Adventure playtest improvements on top of [0.60.1](https://github.com/b0realis/ShandalarGodot/blob/08b2c9af0c3b703db0412d77af54d8bd397ce2ac/docs/releases/0.60.1.md).

- **Quickslot:** Ctrl+S saves your adventure to its own blue Quickslot and
  briefly shows **QUICKSAVED Adventurer!**. Autosaves and all ten named
  slots remain separate.
- **Simpler loading:** one Resume button opens the highlighted save, with
  the newest eligible autosave selected initially. Hall of Fame has a gold
  button at the left; Back sits beside Resume at the right.
- **More trader choices:** Campaign Settings now offers **All cards offered
  by traders**, off by default. It includes cards such as Ifh-Bíff Efreet
  and Serendib Efreet from the adventure pool. Categories, bans and copy
  limits still apply.
- **Fairer shop prices:** selling a card back to the same town cannot pay
  more than its purchase price. Trading between different towns remains
  worthwhile.
- **Clearer adventure controls:** Go is more prominent before a duel, and
  path arrows are smaller.
- **Sylvan Library:** identical drawn cards are offered as separate copies
  in both local and LAN choices.
- **Hand hints:** hovering an unaffordable card no longer makes its name
  yellow; the face still brightens and the card preview still opens.

For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every machine.
