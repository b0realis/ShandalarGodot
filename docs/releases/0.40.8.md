# Shandalar 0.40.8

- Ship the two unchanged 35-card **Portal starter** lists and two clearly
  labelled 40-card adaptations, with exact additions and historical sources
  in the [starter guide](../portal-starters.md).
- Add illustrated strategy pages for both starters to Help, and a generated
  **Help contents** page that links every page; the Portal overview now sits
  with its starter guides instead of inside the abilities chapter.
- Accept **casual deck sizes** of 8–39 cards in local and LAN friendly
  duels, with a warning; eight leaves room for the seven-card opening hand
  and one ante. Gauntlets and LAN tournaments, including their bot seats and
  deck selectors, still require 40. Card-copy rules and upper limits are
  unchanged.
- Correct the Portal overview's stale claim that printing choices were not
  saved.

The LAN compatibility revision distinguishes the casual policy; peers on
either side of it are told to match builds.
