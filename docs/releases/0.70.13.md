# Shandalar 0.70.13

The computer plays its cards better. Thank you to the player who reported these!

## The computer's spells

- **Sandstorm and other combat-only spells are no longer cast outside combat.** The computer now keeps them for your attack and uses them when they are worth a card.
- **Spells that would change nothing stay in hand**: Hurkyl's Recall at a player whose artifacts are not worth a card, a damage shield with nothing to prevent, a sweep of an empty table.
- **Stream of Life and similar X spells wait until X is worthwhile**, unless the computer needs the life now.

## The computer's combat

- **Ball Lightning always attacks.** A creature that is gone at end of turn anyway attacks whenever it can do anything, and is cast before combat, not after it and not into a blocker that stops it cold.
- **The computer no longer throws a creature away to kill an attacker that is sacrificed at end of turn anyway.**
- **Trample.** Facing a lethal trampling attack, the weaker computer opponents now choose the blocks that actually keep them alive.

## Ankh of Mishra, Manabarbs, Power Surge

- **The computer counts the cost of its own Ankh of Mishra**, Manabarbs, Karma, Power Surge and similar cards, and casts one only when it hurts you more than itself. A deck built around them still plays them early.
- **Under Power Surge the computer uses up its lands** before its turn begins where that costs nothing, and values spending its mana more when mana burn is on.
- **Under a land toll the computer plays a land rather than discard a card** when it can afford the life, builds towards the spells in its hand, and stops paying for lands it has no use for.
