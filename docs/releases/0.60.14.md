# Shandalar 0.60.14

- **Every card pack in the adventure.** Tick any of Packs 2–9 on Embark, one
  or all. Each pack adds its own cards to Shandalar's shops, duel rewards and
  lairs at their original rarity (Fifth Edition its reprints): 20% of the
  random cards per pack, at most 60% together, the rest base cards. Choice
  lists — trades, a castle's pick, "any card of your choice" — still offer
  every card.
- **Strong and ante cards.** Strong pack cards such as Necropotence and Force
  of Will are special: never sold in shops, four amulets at a trader. Ante
  cards never stand on a shop shelf.
- Your first deck and the creatures' decks stay base cards. Older adventures
  keep their packs and follow these rules from now on.
- **Tooltips** are opaque everywhere: nothing behind a hint shows through it.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
