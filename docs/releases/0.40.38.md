# Shandalar 0.40.38

- **The End of Duel window folds its lines** — in the gauntlet the
  opponent is named after its deck, and a deck title can run to seventy
  characters; with `See next draws at end of duel` on, the next draw can
  be `The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale`. The window said all of it on
  single lines that walked out of its 272px ground — the title alone
  pushed every line under it out. Every line now wraps at the window's
  edge (the title too), the next draw's card sits on its own row under
  its caption, and the window grows to hold the rows and stays centred.
  The usual window is unchanged at its measured size.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
