# Shandalar 0.50.7

A bug pass over the whole tree, and a second look at the MCP server —
about 150 defects fixed, each with a regression test that failed before
its fix. The full record, with the reproductions and the owner's rulings,
is `docs/bug-pass-2026-10-03.md`.

- **Draws that had a winner.** Both libraries emptied by one Wheel of
  Fortune, or both players killed by one Psionic Blast, Fire and
  Brimstone or Mana Clash, now end in a draw (CR 104.4a, 704.3).
- **Hidden information kept hidden.** The Duel Log window and
  `duel_log.txt` no longer name the AI's Demonic Tutor find or Sylvan
  Library's cards, nor any face-down creature; Clone, Vesuvan
  Doppelganger and Dance of Many copy a masked creature as a nameless
  2/2; the AI no longer reads a masked card's name.
- **A fairer table.** A LAN client can no longer double its combat damage
  by naming one creature twice in its bands; the mana planner pays
  two-colour costs from duals in either pip order; Eureka puts Auras down
  attached, Animate Dead and Dance of the Dead included.
- **A smarter computer player.** It no longer taps pain lands to death,
  lets an unpayable Fog block its other answers, shrinks its own
  creatures, pumps +X/-Y creatures to death, or counters a Llanowar Elves
  with Force of Will.
- **Your files are safe.** Decks and settings are written atomically; a
  hand-edited `settings.cfg` that no longer parses is kept as
  `settings.cfg.bad` instead of being overwritten; the Deck Builder asks
  before discarding unsaved decks.
- **Without the 1997 skin**, the graveyard and exile piles are clickable
  (Raise Dead could not be aimed).
- **LAN.** OK on a friendly duel's result returns to the lobby instead of
  closing it (and, on a host, every other table); a peer can no longer
  pin a gigabyte of the host's memory; a different version is named, not
  a silent hang. The compatibility stamp moved: both players need 0.50.7.
- **Deck Lab.** In-process runs no longer risk heap corruption; a resume
  reads only its own run's games; `--progress json` for programs;
  `--resume` takes `--quiet`/`--no-banner`/`--progress`.
- **MCP server.** A `lab` run without `out` lands in the workspace and is
  read back; `ping` is answered at once and `notifications/cancelled`
  stops a call (its whole process group); `notifications/progress` for
  Lab runs; `lab_resume` works against the real Lab; game numbers are
  claimed on disk.

Gate: 555 scripts, 8,562/8,562 tests, 412,099 asserts, exit 0; Python 484
(8 skipped); duel soak, both rule sets, 24 duels clean.
