# Shandalar 0.50.15

The owner asked for Pack 9, the Tempest block — Tempest, Stronghold and
Exodus — with the engine and the computer opponent updated for its
mechanics. Pack 9 is here.

- **Pack 9 — The Tempest Block.** Tempest, Stronghold and Exodus as one
  optional pack: 636 printings, 621 names, **574 new cards**, all with
  their rules — none is left uncastable. The 47 reprints use the cards
  the game already has (27 of them come with Pack 9 when their own pack
  is off). Build it with `tools/pack_9_tempest_block.py`; turn it on in
  Options → Card Packs. Its badge is 9-TMP; the Deck Builder's Extras
  get a storm, a keep and a bird. Needs 0.50.15.
- **New rules in the engine:** shadow (CR 702.28), both ways, and the
  walls that block "as though it had shadow"; creatures that must block
  (Watchdog, Provoke, Invasion Plans) and Magnetic Web's forced attacks;
  **buyback**, Memory Crystal, Dream Halls' and Aluren's free casting and
  Rootwater Shaman's flash, as payment choices; **licids** that become
  Auras and the "pay to end this effect" action, and Volrath's Curse's
  "sacrifice to ignore"; **Humility as printed** — ability-removing and
  P/T-setting effects now apply in timestamp order, so an ability granted
  after Humility survives; Volrath's Shapeshifter; Ertai's Meddling; "can't
  be countered"; new costs (random discard, a card on top of the library,
  removing a counter); Heartstone; Reap's target count; Pandemonium;
  Silver Wyvern's retargeting; Static Orb; text changes until end of turn;
  control while enchanted. Slivers share their abilities with every Sliver
  on the table, yours and your opponent's.
- **Older cards, fixed on the way:** triggers granted after a silencing
  effect now fire, and older cards' granted abilities (Spectral Cloak,
  Equinox, Torrent of Lava, the Wards, Energy Flux) are ordered against
  Humility by timestamp; Animate Artifact's creature type and size sit in
  their own layers; state-based actions are checked whenever a player
  would receive priority; hand-size effects (Ensnaring Bridge, Maro) are
  judged on the live hand; a sacrificed token's toughness is remembered.
- **The computer opponent** plays the new cards: shadow attacks and the
  tricks before blocks, hostile licids on your creatures and ending a
  licid's effect to save it, buyback when it can afford to recast,
  Dream Halls and Aluren when they save more than they cost, symmetric
  sweepers priced by what each side loses, combat tricks held for their
  moment, Spikes' counters moved when it pays, and Slivers valued on both
  sides of the table.
- **The duel table:** creatures with shadow carry a badge; creatures that
  must block, and the attackers Magnetic Web drags in, are highlighted;
  the licid and Volrath's Curse actions are on the card menus; buyback
  and the free-casting rows are on the payment menu.
- **SGManalink:** every new action works over the network. The protocol
  is **28**; LAN peers need matching 0.50.15 builds.
- **A bug pass over the new mechanics** (the owner: *"First do one bug find
  & bug fix pass over the new mechanics"*): 42 findings, all fixed. The worst
  three: two block requirements sharing a menace partner left no legal block
  declaration; a token Sabertooth Cobra disconnected a network seat; a
  creature that lost its abilities still fired its printed death trigger
  (Personal Incarnation under Humility). A permanent entering without
  abilities now skips its own entry effects (a Spike under Humility is a
  1/1), licid Auras survive a later copy, and every self-harm the computer
  opponent was caught in is fixed. Record: docs/bug-pass-2026-10-06-tempest.md.
- **Help:** shadow and blocking, buyback and free casting, licids,
  Slivers and Spikes, Humility and the block's notable cards, and Pack 9's
  digital adaptations, in plain words.

Gate: 758 scripts, **12,410 tests, 559,259 asserts**, exit 0; Python 608
(15 skipped). Evidence in docs/pack-9-tempest-block.md (acceptance record).
