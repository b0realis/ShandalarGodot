# Shandalar 0.40.37

- **The opponent's hand window floats** — against the computer and across
  the wire the opponent's hand was a fixed plate in a row of their half,
  and it covered whatever lay under it. It is now the same hand window as
  the player's own: dragged by the middle of its bar, its corner
  remembered on its own (`opp_hand_stack_pos`, never the player's
  `hand_stack_pos`), starting where the plate used to be. While the hand
  is hidden it is the title bar alone with the count on it (manual
  p.114); a revealed hand stacks its cards under the bar. The row the
  plate took is given back to the opponent's territory.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
