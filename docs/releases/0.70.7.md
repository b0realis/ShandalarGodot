# Shandalar 0.70.7

- **Places that carry you.** A places plugin's place can now take you
  somewhere: to the nearest town or city, back to where your journey on
  that land began, to another place of the same plugin, or to the dungeon
  it told you of. You are set down on free ground beside it, never on
  water, and if there is no way there the place says so and takes
  nothing.
- **Places that teach.** A place can teach you a World Magic; the seller
  who offered that Magic no longer does, so you never pay for it twice. A
  place can link the mana of the nearest town or city that can still be
  linked, never one a wizard rules or one under siege. And a place can
  raise the life your duels start at, by up to 10 in all from every place
  together: it counts in every duel, dungeons and castles included, your
  hero's page shows it, and it stays yours even if the plugin is later
  taken away.
- **For plugin authors: a wizard.** The authoring tool can now make a
  place by asking you questions with numbered answers: the plugin's name,
  the place's name, which kind of place, its few numbers and names, and
  where it stands. Enter takes the suggested answer, and nothing is
  written before the last one.
- **For plugin authors: a preview page.** One command writes a page, to
  open in any web browser, that shows every pane of your places as the
  game lays it out: the picture, the words, when each line and button
  shows, and what each button does, with the dungeons, opponents and
  Tasks rows after them. It needs no internet.
- **For plugin authors: two more recipes.** A traveler, who carries the
  hero for a fare, and a teacher, who teaches a World Magic once and
  gives three lessons that each add a life. That makes twelve.
- **Two study places repainted.** The Wandering Collector has a new
  picture in her window, and The Duelist's Ring a new picture and a new
  look on the map.
- **Bug fixes.**

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
