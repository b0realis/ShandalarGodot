  playfield showed the name, the P/T, the `(T)`, the counter stones and
  the highlight ring of the card underneath through its face, because a
  `MiniCard`'s parts carried a `z_index` of their own (1 and 2) and z is
  sorted across the whole canvas. Nothing inside a card carries a z now;
  its parts are ordered by the child list alone (the art and its cues,
  then the stones, the dagger, the stripes, the wash and the P/T, then
  the name and the `(T)`, the ring last), so the card touched last
  covers every pixel of the one it lies on.

  lift that answered the aura's ring over its host (2026-09-07) is
  gone; it showed through a card placed over the host the same way.
