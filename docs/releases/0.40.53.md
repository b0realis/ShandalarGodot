# Shandalar 0.40.53

The owner, with the headset's own numbers on the table: *"Can we then
speed load-times (and card-pack discovery) for other platforms as
well?"* — and, from a reader: *"when we make fullscreen card on the
click on large card. Some say the text is a bit blurred?"* Both
measured before anything was changed.

- **Where the 9.1 seconds went.** The Meta Quest start report for
  0.40.52 said `tree ready after 9071 ms`. Read against the log's
  clock: 3.2 s of engine boot before the first line of ours, 0.2 s to
  mount the skin, 1.0 s to seal seven card packs — and then 4.6 s of
  silence before the title. That silence was two compiles the title
  never needed: every card script (`CardRegistry.ensure_loaded()`,
  called on the title's own `_ready` for a year, 0.9 s on the desk)
  and the whole SGManalink script cluster, pulled in because the
  title's script named `SgLobby` (the protocol, tournament and store
  scripts form a cycle the compiler resolves in one go — 1,184 ms of
  the title script's load on the desk, 181 ms without the name).
- **The card pool builds on a thread.** The `CardPacks` autoload
  starts it (`CardRegistry.load_in_background`) the moment the packs
  are configured; the title comes up meanwhile and its version corner
  says `loading cards…` until the frame the thread is done, then the
  count. The first screen that reads a card waits for the thread
  rather than building beside it — any public reader, on any thread —
  and unloading or reconfiguring the packs during a build joins it
  first. The start report gains the line `card pool: N cards in M ms
  (background)`. On the desk under Xvfb the title now stands 0.8 s
  after the process starts; the thread reports 3.4 s while the title
  renders beside it, and nothing waits for that.
- **The screens the buttons open compile in the background too.** Cold
  on the desk the setup screen cost 734 ms (its script names the duel
  screen, and that one the whole duel), the deck builder 276 ms and the
  SGManalink lobby 1,032 ms — on a handheld two to three times that,
  felt as a dead click. Once the menu stands the title hands the
  engine's own loader threads those three (`ScreenWarmup`); a click that
  comes first joins the load in flight, and the process-end hook takes
  every result before static state tears down. Warm, each loads in 0 ms.
- **Card-pack discovery stays as it is.** It is 0.1 s on the desk and
  1.0 s of the headset's 9.1 — the seal read of seven zip files, each
  timed in the report (`sealed, 67..226 ms`) — and it is the engine's
  zip reader doing it. Not worth a change.
- **The fullscreen card's text is sharp.** The reader scales the
  300-px card preview by a node `scale` of about three, and the
  engine's automatic font oversampling follows the window's stretch,
  not a node's scale: a 13 px label rendered at 13 px and stretched
  three times measured 21.4 % intermediate edge pixels (a 39 px label
  rendered at 39 px: 4.8 %). While the reader is open the viewport's
  `oversampling_override` is the automatic value times the card's
  scale (6.2 % — crisp), and closing it restores automatic. One node,
  one property, no second font.
- **What the tests say.** `tests/unit/test_card_registry_background_2026_09_30.gd`
  (8 tests): the background build joined by the first ask, watched by a
  poller without waiting, unloaded and reconfigured under it, the main
  thread loading the very scripts the worker compiles, the title's
  corner saying `loading cards…` then the count, the warm-up asked
  once and settled empty, and beside the pool thread. The Android
  corner's tracer notes the pool's line once, the fullscreen reader's
  test pins the override on open and its release on close.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
