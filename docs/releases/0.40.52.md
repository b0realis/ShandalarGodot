# Shandalar 0.40.52

The owner's AI play gripe: *"Firebreathing card AI play examine — one
AI follow-up remains: duplicate Firebreathing can escape the new
text-based detector. Please fix."* Examined on the desk first: a second
Firebreathing went onto the creature already wearing one, and a wearer
never breathed — unblocked it swung for its printed three with the
Mountains open, blocked it traded. Three faults, all in the AI's
readers and never by a card's name.

- **A second Firebreathing goes on the creature without one.** The
  0.40.47 detector read a card's printed text for a quantity a second
  copy would add, and Firebreathing's `{R}: +1/+0` matched "+1/+0" —
  which is not a quantity the second aura adds: one copy already
  repeats that line for every Mountain. `EffectIntent.stacks` now reads
  the text with every uncapped, untapped, cost-free activated line
  taken out (`_repeatable_text`): Firebreathing and Blessing are had
  once per host; Holy Armor's static `+0/+2` still stacks; a `{T}`
  line, a once-per-turn line and a line paid in a sacrifice keep their
  quantity, as before.
- **The worn breath is breathed.** Every pump path walked only the
  creature's own activated lines, and the aura's `PumpHostEffect` read
  as unknown to the intent reader. The three pump auras declare
  `pump_host` with their numbers, and `AiPlayer._breath_sources` lists
  a body's own lines and — under `pumps_to_attack` — the pump lines of
  the auras it wears (ours, on the battlefield, untapped-cost). An
  unblocked wearer breathes for every open Mountain; a blocked wearer
  buys the one breath that wins the trade and keeps the rest; the
  declaration reader, the reach reader and the block ladder (under
  `reads_pumps`) read THEIR wearer at the size their Mountains reach.
- **The Regeneration aura is a shield.** The 2ed aura's effect now says
  `regenerate_host` / `is_regeneration`, which `_effects_regenerate`
  has read since the Thrull Retainer pass — a blocked wearer is
  shielded before the damage.
- **A test script pins it**: `tests/ai/test_ai_worn_breath_2026_09_30.gd`
  (14 tests) — the second Firebreathing's target, the uncapped line
  read as no quantity, the four breaths unblocked, the one breath
  blocked, the aura named as the source, their wearer's reach, the
  enemy's aura on our body not ours to breathe with, the Regeneration
  shield. Eight of them bit before the fix.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
