- The turn after a Relentless Assault opened with an out-of-bounds read
  of the step: the "== Turn N ==" log line was written while the step
  index still pointed into the twenty-step turn the assault made, and the
  engine printed `Out of bounds get index '19'` every such turn (250
  times in a 439,500-game Deck Lab round; play was not affected, the
  line's step was). The index is put on the new turn's cleanup first,
  the step every other turn's line has always carried.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
