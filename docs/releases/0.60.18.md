# Shandalar 0.60.18

A play-tester's report, answered.

- **Ctrl+L loads your Quickslot at once.** Ctrl+S saves to the Quickslot
  and Ctrl+L takes it up again, with no list in between: on the road, on
  the map and inside a town. Without a Quickslot yet, the game says so and
  you stay where you are. L still opens the saved adventures.
- **Delete saves in the game.** The saved adventures now have a
  **Delete** button beside Back (or press the Delete key). It asks first,
  then deletes the save you chose, or every save of its adventure. The
  Hall of Fame keeps its lines.
- **Go for a creature.** Over a creature you can see, the pointer is now a
  small sword: click and your hero goes after it, the way found again as it
  moves, until you meet. Walking straight into a creature, with the keys
  or a click, now meets it too, instead of walking up beside it.
- **Hi-res cursors.** The game's own pointers — the adventure's walker,
  places and sword, and the duel's targeting crosshair — are now twice the
  1997 size and sharp. **Options → Hi-res cursors** switches back to the
  1997 size.
- **Clicks and arrow keys together.** An arrow key pressed against a walk
  turns your hero on the spot instead of finishing the step first. A click
  during a walk goes on through the tile ahead instead of stepping back. A
  key still held through a click or Space no longer turns the next key
  into a diagonal. The path preview no longer pops up after the arrow keys
  stop, until you move the mouse, and a mouse button let go after an arrow
  key walks nowhere.
- **The card shop is heard.** Selling a card in a town plays the sound of
  gold, and buying one plays the sound of the card you found.
- **Card art downloads on Windows.** When Windows refuses the picture
  server's certificate with "certificate has expired", the tools try once
  more trusting only Windows' own root certificates, which fixes it. If
  the certificate still can't be checked, the tools stop at once with what
  to do instead of warning for every card. Downloads that already worked
  are unchanged.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
