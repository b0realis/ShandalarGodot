# Shandalar 0.40.26

- The AI reads the three conscriptions — Nettling Imp, Norritt and
  Arcum's Whistle order a creature of the active player's to attack this
  turn or be destroyed — and activates them at their beginning of
  combat, the last priority before attackers are declared: against the
  body it is worth most against, priced the way a Siren's Call is (a
  creature that cannot attack is destroyed at their end step; one that
  must is met by the best blocker of ours that kills it), the Whistle's
  ransom read through their open mana, never into a swing that already
  kills us. Sorcerer and Wizard, under `casts_timed_spells`.
- The prison, read. Four new knobs on Sorcerer and Wizard, each read off
  a printed line or a shape the card declares, nothing card-named in a
  decision: `pays_the_rent` keeps the {U} a Stasis charges at its own
  upkeep while the freeze holds the mana, casts a Stasis only when the
  lock favours us and its first rent is in hand, and lets a losing
  Stasis go at the upkeep rather than pay for it; `buys_life` spends a
  land under a Zuran Orb (a Forest under a Dark Heart of the Wood) for
  the life that turns a lethal swing into a survived one, as their
  attack is declared and no earlier; `swaps_life` trades life totals
  through a Mirror Universe at our upkeep when theirs is the larger;
  `takes_the_turn` taps an untapped Time Vault for its turn and answers
  "Skip this turn to untap?" off the board — a dead turn banked, a live
  one played — instead of the 1997 one-in-five roll. Maze of Ith untaps
  an attacker out of their combat once the blocks are known
  (`casts_timed_spells`); Transmute Artifact waits for an artifact of
  ours to sacrifice (`holds_duplicates`). Measured in the Lab, Wizard
  against Wizard at 1,000 games an arm: `pays_the_rent` +7.7 ±2.4,
  `swaps_life` +10.4 ±3.1, `buys_life` +2.9 ±2.7, the Maze +17.2 ±4.2,
  `takes_the_turn` +3.2 ±4.2. `docs/ai-difficulty.md` has the rows.
