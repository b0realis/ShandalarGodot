# Shandalar 0.40.41

The Steam Deck release — *"do 1-4 along with pad navigable duel along
with one button dedicated to advance game stage."*

- **A controller plays the table.** The new `PadControls` layer turns a
  pad into the mouse the 1997 screens were written for: left stick
  moves a pointer, the D-pad hops it to the nearest card or button in
  that direction, A is the left button (hold and move to drag, two
  quick presses to auto-cast), LB the right button (the mini-menus),
  the right stick the wheel. Every other button reaches the duel as
  before: X is Done, B cancels (and closes a mini-menu), Y the hand,
  Start the pause menu, Back the log. **Options → Display → Pad
  pointer**: `Auto` (on while a controller is connected), `On`, `Off`.
  A real mouse motion takes the pointer back at once.
- **RB is the one button.** The pad default of `duel_space` — the
  Situation Bar's only button, the advance — moved from A to RB, so A
  can be the click. Rebindable in Options → Controls as before; a
  player who never rebound gets RB by themselves.
- **The menus walk with the D-pad.** The shell, Options, the battle
  setup and the gauntlet's startup window start the engine's focus
  ring on their first button (the keyboard's arrows too), and the
  pad's A presses the focused button and B cancels — Godot's `ui_accept`
  and `ui_cancel` were keyboard-only.
- **Handheld defaults.** `packaging/handhelds/steam-deck.sh` and
  `arkos.sh` export `SHANDALAR_HANDHELD`; under it, three settings the
  player has never changed open on a handheld's defaults — full
  screen, full-screen card on click and the power saver on. An Options
  choice always wins and nothing is written until one is made.
- **Power saver** (Options → Display): the engine's low-processor mode,
  redrawing only on change and resting between frames. Off by default
  on a desktop.
- A launcher's own `--fullscreen` (ArkOS passes it) is no longer undone
  at boot by an unwritten window mode.
- Nothing is verified on Deck hardware yet; `docs/handhelds.md` says so.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
