# Shandalar 0.40.27

- The AI's reading of the damage that is coming, once blocks are in,
  now runs the engine's own division of each attacker's power over its
  blockers (`AiPlayer._declared_damage`, `MtgGame.default_damage_split`):
  a blocked trampler's surplus lands on us, a trampler whose blockers
  all died lands the whole of it, a defensive band denies the spill, a
  body that assigns no combat damage lands nothing. The first cut summed
  the unblocked power alone, so an 8/8 Force of Nature through a 2/2
  Grizzly Bears read as 0 while the engine dealt 6 — a Zuran Orb stayed
  untapped at five life against it, and the Maze of Ith's lethal check
  had the same gap. Combat itself was always right; only the AI's
  estimate was short. `tests/ai/test_ai_trample_lands_on_us_2026_09_26.gd`
  pins the Bears, the Orb and the Maze.
- Two documents corrected: `docs/pack-7-fifth-edition.md` and
  `docs/pack-6-portal.md` described a browser card-pack upload flow the
  web build does not offer — the numbered packs are desktop-only, as
  `docs/card-art-and-packs.md` says; `docs/release-builds.md` counted
  five bundled numbered-pack builders where the package carries seven.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
