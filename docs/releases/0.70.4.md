# Shandalar 0.70.4

- **New places in the adventure.** Two plugins come with the game,
  each a place of its own. *An Old Adventurer's Hut* stands in the
  mainland's woods: the old man inside, once a planeswalker of another
  world, tells you for 1000 gold where a dungeon lies that nobody else
  knows of, and it rises on your map with its treasure. The *Conjuration
  Wizard Guild* has a tower on the mainland and on every island you sail
  to: its conjurers copy a card you carry for 2000 gold, then 4000, 8000
  and 12000, and after that never again. Tick them at the Embark under
  "Expanded adventure plugins...".
- **Places plugins.** Plugin authors can now make places, not only
  islands: a picture, a map sprite, panes of words and services with
  prices, and dungeons of their own cards. The authors' guide has a new
  page on them, and the authoring tool new commands: start a places
  plugin, add a place or a dungeon, play a place through as text, and
  check its pictures. Saves are written in a new version; older saves load as
  before.
- **The tutor's list shows its cards.** The list of your library that a
  tutor such as Vampiric Tutor opens now has the large card on its left:
  the card of the name under the pointer, or of the line you are on.
- **The arrow keys walk every selection window of the duel.** In a
  question, a tutor's list of your library, a spell's modes and a LAN
  table's menus, Up and Down walk the lines, round from the last to the
  first, and Enter takes the one that is lit. Nothing is lit as a window
  opens, so a Space pressed to pass never answers a question that pops up.
  With the gamepad pointer off, the D-pad walks them too. In the Sideboard
  window between duels, the keys stay on the same line after a card moves.
- **Shandalar opens Embark at once.** The main menu's Shandalar goes
  straight to Embark, without the "still in development" notice first.

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
