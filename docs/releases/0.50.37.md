# Shandalar 0.50.37

Fixes on top of 0.50.34, the published release.

- **Cards can be moved right to the edge of the table.** A card you drag
  around the battlefield used to stop short of the edges: a little short
  at the top and sides, and well short of the bottom. Now it goes all the
  way, so its edge touches the edge of the table, on your side and on
  your opponent's. A tapped card parked against an edge stays whole
  while it is turned, and goes back to exactly where you put it when it
  untaps.

## From 0.50.36

- **LAN hosting is calmer under attack.** A LAN connection that sends
  more than 4 KB of junk before joining is let go straight away, even when
  the host's computer is busy. Before, a busy host could print an error
  line every frame for a second and a half before letting it go.

## From 0.50.35

- **The life panel keeps its picture's shape.** The picture behind your
  life total, and the duelist's face the panel turns over to, were
  stretched to fill the panel, which is wider than the 1997 one, so the
  skull, the crystal ball and the green leaf frame came out nearly twice
  as wide as drawn. Now the picture meets the panel halfway: it is
  widened only a little and loses only a little of its top and bottom,
  so it fills the panel and keeps its look.
