# Shandalar 0.70.9

- **Card packs, made in the game.** Options has a new purple button,
  **Get Card Packs...**, beside **Card Packs...**. Its window lists the
  1997 card pictures and Packs 1 to 10 with what each needs; tick the
  ones you want and press OK. The game downloads the card pictures from
  Scryfall, builds each pack, checks it and puts it in your card folder,
  with a progress bar and a time left. Stop keeps what was downloaded,
  and choosing the pack again goes on from there. Nothing else needs to
  be installed. Not in the browser version, which has no card folder.
- **A skin from your own 1997 game, made in the game.** Under the skin in
  Options, **Build from a 1997 install...** reads your own copy of the
  1997 game — the folder it was installed to, its CD, or a CD image
  (**Choose CD image...**: .iso, or .bin with its .cue), read in place
  with nothing to mount or unpack — says what it found there, and builds the skin and puts it on in well under a minute;
  the game wears it from the next start. It is the same skin the supplied
  tool makes, without the coin-toss and ending movies: the game shows its
  own coin toss, and the ending plays without its film. The window says
  so and points to the Python tool that comes with the game
  (`tools/mtg_assets.py`), which makes the complete skin with both. On
  Windows, Linux and macOS.
- **A game with no 1997 skin says so.** Once a start, the title says the
  game is drawing plain panels of its own and where to make the classic
  skin for the unmistakable look. **Don't show again** silences it.
- **The old adventurer has more to tell.** In *An Old Adventurer's Hut*,
  when you come back from the dungeon he sold you the way to, the old
  man offers another: five in all, one after another, for 1000 gold
  each — The Forgotten Vault, The Drowned Library, The Sunken Forge, The
  Hall of Echoes and The Planeswalker's Rest — each with treasure of its
  own, and each free to lie farther from his hut than the last, across the whole mainland. An
  adventure that already cleared the Vault finds his next offer waiting.
- **For plugin authors: more room.** A place may have 16 panes (it was
  8).
- **The skin's Choose... button is gold** in Options.
- **Masticore lets you choose the card.** When Masticore's upkeep asked
  for a discard, the game took a card of its own choosing; now you choose
  it. The same goes for six more cards that ask for a sacrifice, a
  discard or lands returned "unless" at upkeep: you choose which. Found
  with the help of a player, Chris. Thank you!

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
