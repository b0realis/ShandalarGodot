# Shandalar 0.50.11

*"Can we create a new pack 8 of cards, the same way as last packs were
done. It will be “The Mirage Block”"* — the owner. Pack 8 is here.

- **Pack 8 — The Mirage Block.** Mirage, Visions and Weatherlight as one
  optional pack: 684 printings, 669 names, **621 new cards**, all with
  their rules — none is left uncastable. The 48 reprints use the cards
  the game already has (31 of them come with Pack 8 when their own pack
  is off). Build it with `tools/pack_8_mirage_block.py`; turn it on in
  Options → Card Packs. Its badge is 8-MIR; the Deck Builder's Extras
  get a palm, an eye and a skyship. Needs 0.50.11.
- **New rules in the engine:** phasing (CR 702.26) as a keyword, at the
  untap step, with Auras riding along and phased-out permanents drawn
  faded on the table; flanking; flash and Mirage's "as though it had
  flash" rider; the cleanup step's triggers now come after the discard
  and the damage removal, with a chance to respond (which also fixes
  Bounty of the Hunt and Thawing Glaciers); bans that reach mana
  abilities (Null Rod stops a Mox in auto-pay too); new costs
  (return to hand, exile from hand or graveyard, X objects, alternative
  costs); damage replacements over any victim with the affected player
  ordering them; colour outside the battlefield (Celestial Dawn); turn
  and untap skips; and untap-step triggers ordered together with the
  upkeep's.
- **Older cards, fixed on the way:** an Aura that says "creature you
  control" (or "an opponent controls") falls off when control changes;
  "additional block" permissions add up; "loses <keyword>" removes every
  copy; 104 places in older cards that remembered a permanent now treat
  a phased-out one as gone; ability menu text no longer reads "{R}, :".
- **The computer opponent** plays the new cards: phasing as removal and
  as a save, flash creatures as ambushes, the flash Auras as combat
  tricks, damage shields as responses, alternative costs for the win,
  and lands it would lose to their own entry cost stay in hand.
- **SGManalink:** every new action works over the network (casting from
  a graveyard, Three Wishes, Circling Vultures, phased-out permanents,
  Heat Wave's block cost). The protocol is **26**; LAN peers need
  matching 0.50.11 builds.
- **Help:** phasing, flanking, flash, the cleanup window, the new costs
  and the Mirage block's five digital adaptations, in plain words.

Gate: 635 scripts, **10,205 tests, 473,522 asserts**, exit 0; Python 508
(8 skipped). Evidence in docs/pack-8-mirage-block.md (acceptance record).
