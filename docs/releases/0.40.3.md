# Shandalar 0.40.3

- **AutoDeck:** build from sets, sealed pools or pasted lists; choose colours, rarity, lands and Power Nine. Sample hands and mana-base analysis included.
- **LAN duels and tournaments:** open tables with click-to-join; optional invitation-only hosting; clearer lobby and tournament controls.
- **Duel improvements:** rebindable keyboard/controller controls, attack banding, free combat-damage division, mana-payment fixes and clearer arrows/tooltips. Gold Roman II emblem for Unlimited.
- **Packs 1–5:** construction tools and metadata in every game ZIP. No card pictures or constructed packs are distributed.

## Downloads

Apple Silicon Mac, Intel Mac, Windows x86-64, Linux x86-64, Raspberry Pi 5 ARM64 and Web; each plain or `-with-skin`. Separate `original_skin.zip` and **SHA256SUMS** included.

Extract the whole folder. Put skin/card-art ZIPs in `skin/`, numbered packs in `cardpacks/`. See **CARD-ART-AND-PACKS.md** for construction commands. Serve Web over HTTP; gameplay packs and LAN are desktop-only.

LAN requires matching **0.40.3 builds and enabled packs** (protocol 21). Mac apps are ad-hoc signed, not notarized; Windows binaries are unsigned.
