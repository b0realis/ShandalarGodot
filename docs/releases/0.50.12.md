# Shandalar 0.50.12

*"Then do one pass over Mirage block and find bugs and fix them!"* — the
owner, after 0.50.11. Eight hunters went through Pack 8 and proved 46
bugs with failing probes; the fixers confirmed or found seven more. They
are fixed; the few left open are listed in
[bug-pass-2026-10-04-mirage.md](../bug-pass-2026-10-04-mirage.md).

- **The computer opponent no longer kills itself** with Final Fortune
  (cast like Time Walk), Infernal Contract, Reign of Terror, Pygmy Hippo's
  mana under the 1997 rules, or a Karoo's spare mana at 1 life. It also
  stops wasting Tidal Wave, Three Wishes, Waiting in the Weeds, Zombie Mob
  and Goblin Grenadiers, and keeps the right card when a card makes it
  lose one.
- **You choose your lands' entry payments.** Lotus Vale, Scorched Ruins and
  the Alliances entry lands ask which lands to sacrifice; declining puts the
  land into the graveyard as printed, and the line says so; the overlay's
  Cancel takes the land play back.
- **Paying from your territory menu:** Sabertooth Cobra's ransom, Channel's
  life for mana and Guardian Angel's paid prevention, on the local screen.
- **Rules:** cumulative upkeep does nothing for a phased-out permanent;
  Peace Talks covers the turn that really comes next; a stolen permanent is
  not sacrificed by its thief at the end step; Teferi's Veil, Glyph of Doom,
  Infinite Authority and Time Elemental use the stack at end of combat; Null
  Rod, Cursed Totem and Helm of Awakening stop while tapped under the 1997
  rules or silenced; Hall of Gemstone, Ward of Lights, Chaosphere, Celestial
  Dawn's Teferi's Isle and land-type order are right; +1/+1 and -1/-1
  counters cancel under the modern presets; "whenever it is dealt damage"
  triggers once per damage event; a spell cast from a graveyard obeys Dense
  Foliage and can't target itself.
- **Fair play:** the tutor search no longer shows your library in its order.
- **The screens:** spells with an additional cost you can't pay are not lit;
  X for "X targets" stops at the legal targets; small fixes for Circling
  Vultures, the graveyard ring and Heat Wave's note; ability menus name
  every cost and state their limits once; "you gain 1 life".
- **SGManalink protocol 27**: Heat Wave's block costs can no longer overflow
  a networked view, and a view the host can't send is reported, not dropped.
  LAN players need matching 0.50.12 builds.

Gate: 644 scripts, **10,409 tests, 479,524 asserts**, exit 0; Python 508
(8 skipped); 180 audit duels, 24 UI soak duels and a Linux export's
real-ZIP probe clean.
