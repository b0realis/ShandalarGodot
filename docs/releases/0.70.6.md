# Shandalar 0.70.6

- **Places that do more.** A places plugin's place could sell a service
  for gold, copy a card and tell you of a dungeon. Now a place can also
  talk with you over several pages, with questions as buttons; gamble
  with you on a throw of the dice; ask for a card, for amulets or for
  food as its price; give you a card, amulets or a gift for your next
  duel; duel you for a wager of gold against an opponent of its own; buy
  and sell cards; and add a row to your Tasks with an arrow on the map.
  A place tells you why before it takes anything: with a full bag, no
  deck or too little of what it asks, nothing is paid. A throw of a
  place's dice comes out the same after a reload, and a wagered duel you
  leave unfinished is lost with the wager paid.
- **Four study places come with the game.** In The Gambler's Den, Odo
  Three-Fingers throws bone dice for 50 gold a throw. The Riddle Stone
  asks one riddle and pays 100 gold for the right answer. The Wandering
  Collector pays 150 gold for each Ornithopter you hand over. In The
  Duelist's Ring, Marda Ironhand duels for a wager of 50 gold. Each is a
  plugin of its own, switched on or off at the Embark under Expanded
  adventure plugins..., and none needs a card pack. Left unticked, they
  change nothing: the adventure is exactly the one you know.
- **For plugin authors: recipes.** The authoring tool writes a whole
  place from one of ten recipes: a place of lore, a gift, a healer, a
  toll gate, a gambler, a riddle, a collector, a duelist, a shop or an
  errand. Each plays as written, and its sentences are yours to rewrite.
- **For plugin authors: small edit commands.** A button, a pane, a flag,
  a counter, an opponent with a deck, a sentence or the plugin's name and
  description is each added with one command, so no file has to be opened
  by hand. A change that the game would refuse is not written.
- **For plugin authors: a better walk-through.** The tool's text
  walk-through of a place can now be told what the hero carries and which
  buttons to press, can take one hero across the mainland and an island,
  and lists what it never reached, with what stood in the way. The check
  of a plugin warns of what is not finished yet, and suggests the nearest
  name for a mistyped card or text.
- **For plugin authors: a cheat sheet.** The authors' guide has a new
  page with every word a place can use on one line each: what to write
  and the command that writes it. The page on places explains the new
  words with the four study places as examples. A place that uses a new
  word needs this version of the game; an older game lists it as needing
  a newer one and plays on without it.
- **Places plugin fixes.** A place that stands on the mainland and on the
  islands now sells its dungeon once on each land; before, buying it on
  the mainland took the offer away everywhere. A place can no longer trap
  you: a plugin whose place has no page to open on, or a page without a
  way to leave, is not loaded. Paying again for a dungeon that still
  waits for you is refused. Plugin zips are checked more strictly, and
  one that is not a plain zip is refused before anything is unpacked.
- **Bug fixes.**

Measured and tested. Existing skins, card packs and adventure saves remain
usable. For LAN games, use matching game versions and card packs on every
machine.
