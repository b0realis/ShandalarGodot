# Shandalar 0.40.48

The Meta Quest 3 package: the game as a flat panel app in the headset,
sideloaded over adb.

- **A signed Android APK** — `./build_release.sh --quest` exports the new
  `Android Quest` preset (one arm64 APK on the prebuilt template, package
  `com.b0realis.shandalar`, no gradle build, no XR mode) and signs it with
  a key that lives beside the build tree, never in the repository;
  `tools/package_release.py --platform meta-quest` wraps it with the
  usual documents and, in the `-with-skin` ZIP, `skin/original_skin.zip`.
  No launcher and no headless doors: a headset runs nothing beside the
  app.
- **The player's folders on Android** sit in the app's own corner of the
  shared storage, `/sdcard/Android/data/com.b0realis.shandalar/files/`
  — `cardpacks/`, `portraits/`, `music/` and `skin/`, the desktop play
  copy's layout one level down — where `adb push` writes and the app
  reads without a permission; the private `user://` folder, which
  nothing outside the app can fill, keeps the settings, the decks and
  the tournament checkpoints (`GamePaths.android_files_dir`,
  `player_place`; `GameSkin.portable_dir_for`; `CardPacks` looks there
  too). On a desk nothing moves.
- **An Android build is its own handheld**: `Settings.handheld` says
  `android` where no launcher named a device, so full screen, the
  click-to-enlarge card and the power saver open on, as on the Deck.
- **The back gesture is Escape**, one press and release through the
  input door, never a quit (`Lifecycle`, `quit_on_go_back` off).
- `docs/handhelds.md` has the Quest section: Developer Mode, the three
  adb lines, where the files live, Unknown Sources.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
