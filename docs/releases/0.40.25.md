# Shandalar 0.40.25

- The AutoDeck builder fits its land count to the curve the fill made
  (Frank Karsten's 2022 regression over 95,143 decklists, settled within
  two of the speed's own count), splits its basics by the pips and mana
  values the spells need to be cast on time, marks a card down for every
  source of its colour the lands will not give, chooses its colours on
  the mana they would be laid, drops a splash the lands cannot carry
  (fewer than four cards in 60, three in 40 — unless asked for, kept, or
  a gold deck's second colour) and fills the slots again, gives a colour
  asked for its quota of cards, casts an X spell for two more than its
  printed cost and prices it so, and reads a card's strings — a creature
  that enters asking for a card or a permanent, a coin flip, a spell
  that asks for a Goblin. The report says which of these happened. An
  A/B of 45 decks against the tournament gauntlet: 45.4% to the 0.40.24
  builder's 43.7%, mono-red held.
- A Variety row in the AutoDeck window — Best, A little, Some, Wild —
  is how far the seed's taste moves a card's worth, so two seeds build
  two decks rather than one with a card or two changed; the wish is
  remembered and named in the report.
- The AutoDeck CLI: `--colors =WU` is exactly those colours; `mono`,
  `pairs`, `triples`, `quads`, `five` and `every` are every colour set
  of that size as alternatives the walk deals equally; `random` draws
  exactly; `--variety` is an axis; `--distinct 60` builds every deck
  again until it is 60% different by cards from every earlier deck of
  the run; `--keep FILE --vary "Fireball, 2 Lightning Bolt"` holds a
  deck but for the named cards and fills their slots from the pool, so
  the Lab can find a better card for a slot with the held deck as the
  control. `decks.csv` gains `variety` and `distinct`.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
