# Shandalar 0.50.4

A game played through the MCP server against the Wizard turned up the
missing half of banding: a creature that blocked a White Knight + Pikemen
band was let kill both, when the attacking player divides that blocker's
damage and would have kept the Knight.

- **Offensive banding (CR 702.22j).** When a creature blocks an attacking
  band, the *attacking* player now divides that blocker's combat damage
  among the band's members, freely — the 1997 game's `Assign damage to
  attackers` pass. Until now the blocker's controller spread it
  lethal-first down the band and buried as many members as the points
  reached, which `engine/combat.gd` had called an approximation of the
  rule. The engine's own answer for a seat that does not choose is the
  sacrificial lamb: the whole packet on the member it minds losing least.
  Defensive banding (a banding blocker handing the attacker's division
  to the defender) is unchanged. The duel screen's prompt and the log
  read `<blocker>: Assign damage to attackers, N points left` for the
  pass; the AI's damage forecast runs the engine and follows.
