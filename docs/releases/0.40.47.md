# Shandalar 0.40.47

The third Steam Deck playtest report, answered: a graveyard view that
stays up, a card in it that can be targeted, and an AI that stops
casting a card it already has out.

- **The graveyard and exile view stays open.** It sits over every
  window of the duel — the chain box, a card in flight, the hand window,
  the log, the combat window — and under the Situation Bar, whose Cancel
  is the way out of a cast the pile was opened for (`GraveyardView.Z`).
  A double click on the plate — the second press arriving with the
  click that opened it — closes nothing; the dim closes on the RELEASE
  of a press it took, the way every button does; and a **Done** button
  in the 1997 button art stands bottom-centre under the shelves, for
  the pad and the touchscreen.
- **Raise Dead targets through the view.** With a spell that targets a
  graveyard card pending, a click on the ringed plate opens the pile on
  the page with the first legal card, that card wears the target ring,
  and a click on it takes the target and closes the view — driven in
  the tests with the same mouse events the engine makes from a real
  click, plate, card, Cancel over the dim and the double click alike.
- **The AI never casts the same card twice.** *"It played the same aura
  card (Regeneration) on the card with already the same aura on it"*:
  a second Regeneration on the creature already wearing one, a second
  Kismet or Winter Orb beside the first, is a card thrown away. The
  reader tells a card that STACKS — a pump, a counter, a mana or damage
  or life quantity, a copy — from one that only sets a rule, and the AI
  holds the repeat in hand (`EffectIntent.stacks`, `aura_repeats`,
  `permanent_repeats`; `AiProfile.holds_repeats`, on for every
  difficulty, off in the exact old null).
- `docs/handhelds.md` describes the Done button and the view under the
  bar; `docs/ai-difficulty.md` names the knob.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
