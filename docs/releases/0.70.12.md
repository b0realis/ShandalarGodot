# Shandalar 0.70.12

Four fixes from one player's report. Thank you!

## Cards

- **Lands no longer wear the artifact frame.** Strip Mine, Mishra's Factory, Library of Alexandria, Maze of Ith and the other lands that make colorless mana or none now wear a land frame. With the 1997 skin they wear the original land frames of Arabian Nights, Antiquities, Legends, The Dark, Fallen Empires and Ice Age; City of Brass wears the Arabian Nights one. A skin made before this version shows a grey land frame until you build it again (**Options → Build from a 1997 install…**).

## Duel

- **The computer no longer kills itself with lands under Ankh of Mishra.** It does not play or fetch a land when the damage would be its last, and it holds lands back when the damage is a large share of its life unless the land lets it cast something. It also stops tapping lands when Manabarbs would deal the last points.

## Adventure

- **A town that asks for a card no longer takes one as you walk in.** You now choose which of your matching cards to give, seeing how many you own, the decks that hold it, its rarity and price, or keep them all with "Not now" and come back later. While the quest runs the town offers "Give a card for the quest", and the quest offer says up front that one of your cards will be given away.
- **Serendib Efreet and trades.** Like the 1997 game, traders keep back a few cards (Serendib Efreet, Juzám Djinn and others); they still turn up in shops and duel winnings. **All cards offered by traders** on the Embark screen lifts this for a new adventure.

## Decks

- **A new community deck: Atog Aggro**, a player's own Shandalar deck.
