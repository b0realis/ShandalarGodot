# Shandalar 0.40.39

- **The opponent chooser fires as the Vise enters** — *"As Black Vise
  enters, choose an opponent"* was decided silently at a two-seat table
  (there is one opponent to name), so a Vise that arrived without a word
  read as a Vise that did nothing. The question is now put to the caster
  — the choice window with the opponent's name as its one line — for
  Black Vise, The Rack and Cursed Rack alike; the AI takes the hint
  without a pause, and the upkeep squeeze is unchanged.
- **The AI keeps Berserk for the kill** — it used to cast Berserk in its
  own main phase at the opponent's best creature (on turn one, at the
  opponent's first creature), where the "destroy it if it attacked"
  clause could never fall: a gift of +X/+0 and trample. Berserk is now
  cast in one place only — after blocks, on an attacker of the AI's own
  whose doubled power, or whose trample excess over its blocker, ends
  the game on that attack.
- No wire change: protocol 24 peers interoperate.
