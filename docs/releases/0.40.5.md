# Shandalar 0.40.5

- On-card set symbols now show rarity: white common, silver uncommon,
  gold rare, purple legendary or mythic.
- Legendary status takes precedence; reprints use their displayed set's
  rarity. Set-filter medallions are unchanged.
- Original dual lands gain procedural two-color, nested rules-text panels
  in enlarged card views, with or without the original skin.
- Reverse Damage works in the classic prevention window; pending damage
  keeps its source selectable after the spell resolves, and the AI can
  use Reverse Damage there without spending duplicate shields.
- Expanding a card's text preserves the selected reprint and rarity symbol.
