# Shandalar 0.50.5

The MCP seat learns to pass on its own and to stop where a real player
reaches for a card; a duel survives the client that started it; a view
of what moved; a LAN table joined by name; the journal kept for review.

- **Pass-until.** `referee_act {action, until}` sends the answer and
  passes priority up to the seat's own `main` phase, this turn's `end`
  step, the seat's next `turn`, its next main phase with something to
  `play`, or only a reaction window (`respond`) — and every value stops
  where a player reacts: the opponent's spell or ability on the stack,
  their declared attackers, their blocks, first-strike damage and end
  step while the seat holds something, the seat's own block decision,
  and every attack, block, discard, damage and choice of its own. The
  answer carries `stop` (why) and `passed` (how many decisions).
- **The kept game.** `referee_start`/`referee_join` with `keep` (a join
  is kept by default) leave the referee listening on a loopback socket
  in its own session (`DeckLab/referee.sh --listen FILE --idle SECONDS`;
  the token is drawn by the referee and written to the handshake file
  only). A client restarted lists what waits with `referee_resume {}`
  and takes a game up with `referee_resume {game}`: `hello` again, a
  `resume` line with the counters, the awaited decision with the whole
  journal. A decision nobody came back for in 30 minutes is conceded
  (`reason: idle`). Referee protocol stays 1.
- **The delta view.** `view: "delta"` renders only what moved since the
  last answer — life with `life_was`, the hand's and battlefields'
  additions, changes and removals, the graveyards' additions, the stack,
  the castable names, the new journal lines.
- **A table by name.** `referee_join {table: "Kitchen"}` (`--table NAME`)
  finds an open LAN table through discovery and joins with the invitation
  its advert carries. At a table `--log FILE` (`referee_join {log}`) now
  writes the journal the seat saw.
- **The guide.** `agentic-playgude-mtg.md` carries the first game's
  lessons in chapter 3 ("Driving a duel through the referee"), the
  journal review in chapter 13 and two recovery rows in chapter 14.
- **Banding, documented.** The guide's banding paragraph and
  `docs/mechanics.md` name both divisions of CR 702.22 after 0.50.4.
