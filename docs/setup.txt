SHANDALAR — SETUP
=================

A remake of MicroProse's Magic: The Gathering (1997), built in Godot.

The game runs the moment you unzip it. Everything below is about making
it LOOK like 1997 — which is optional, in the strict sense that with no
art at all every panel, button, card and portrait falls back to a drawn
equivalent of the same size, and nothing is unreachable.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RUN IT
---------------------------------------------------------------------

    ./run.sh                 (or: ./Shandalar.x86_64)

Linux, 64-bit. No installer, no dependencies, nothing written outside
your home directory.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------------------------------------------------------

    Shandalar.x86_64     the game
    Shandalar.pck        its code, the 897 cards' rules, and 317 decks
    skin/                the art's folder, EMPTY BUT FOR THE CATALOGUE:
                         the art is not in this zip (see below)
    skin/SKIN.txt        the catalogue: every file a skin holds, its
                         format, size and name, for drawing a skin of
                         your own
    icon.png             the game's icon
    shortcut.sh          puts the game, with its icon, in your
                         application menu — only if you run it
                         (./shortcut.sh --remove takes it out again)
    run.sh               a launcher that sets the working directory
    deck_lab.sh          headless deck testing (section 6)
    DECKLAB.md           its manual
    setup.txt            this file

The .pck holds no art of any kind, and neither does this zip: the game
is one download, the art another. Art is read off the filesystem at
runtime — out of a zip, or loose files beside it — which is what makes
the skin swappable: drop another skin zip onto the game's window and it
is kept (section 3). The two zips the game wears:

    original_skin.zip    the 1997 art (84 MB) — a separate download
                         where you got this one, or already in skin/
                         if yours is the "-with-skin" zip. Put it in
                         skin/ beside the game, or choose it in
                         Options > Skin, or drop it on the window; the
                         game reads it in place, never unpacked.
    cardart.zip          the card pictures (193 MB). NOT downloadable
                         here — the pictures are Scryfall's, on their
                         own licence — but built in a few minutes by
                         the two scripts in this folder (section 4).
                         Goes in skin/ beside the game the same way, or
                         in the card folder.

Without the first the game wears its own plain skin; without the second
every card draws with a clean placeholder. Both are playable.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EVERY PATH THE GAME READS OR WRITES
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Your own files live in Godot's user directory. On Linux that is:

    ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Shandalar/

and inside it:

    settings.cfg              every option you change — and the five
                              places below, if you move them (see
                              THE PLACES ARE YOURS TO MOVE)
    decks/                    decks you save        (*.deck)
    decks/export/             decks exported for the 1997 program (*.dck)
    portraits/                your own portraits    (*.png, *.jpg, *.webp)
    music/                    your own music        (*.wav, *.ogg, *.mp3)
    original_skin/            the skin folder: a 1997 skin as loose files
    skins/                    skin zips you chose or dropped on the game
                              (original_skin.zip by default; a zip keeps
                              its own name)
    cardpacks/                the card folder: every zip in it is worn
    logs/                     crash and error logs

ART IS SEARCHED IN FOUR PLACES, FIRST MATCH WINS:

    1. the skin folder                   original_skin/ above
    2. <this folder>/skin/               loose files beside the game
    3. the mounted zips                  your skin zip, then every card
                                         pack, then <this folder>/skin/
                                         original_skin.zip and cardart.zip
                                         if you put them there
    4. (a development checkout's assets/original/, not present here)

So anything you put in (1) or (2) overrides what shipped in (3). Portraits
and music are searched slightly differently, because your own come first
AND ARE ADDED TO THE 1997 ONES, not put in their place: a face you drop
in portraits/ appears in the portrait chooser beside the sixty-odd
originals, a tune in music/ plays among the original scores.

    portraits:  portraits/ -> original_skin/portraits/ -> skin/portraits/ -> the zips'
    music:      music/     -> original_skin/           -> skin/           -> the zips'

THE ZIPS are read where they lie; nothing is unpacked, ever. To wear a
different skin, CHOOSE ITS ZIP IN OPTIONS > SKIN (the Skin row says
which zip dresses the game now, by its path, and its Choose... opens a
file box) or DROP THE ZIP ONTO THE GAME'S WINDOW: it is copied into
skins/ under its own name and worn from then on — on the title screen
the new art shows at once; anywhere else the game says the art is in
and offers a restart. Your zip takes precedence over the one that
shipped; choose another to replace it, or delete it from skins/ (the
row shows the path; in a browser, where there is no folder to open,
the screen has a "Forget my zips" button instead) to go back to what
shipped. A zip must have a `skin/` folder at
its top and everything inside that folder, or it is refused whole (the
game says so). SKIN.txt lists what goes in; `python3 skin_catalogue.py
--check my_skin.zip` reports what a zip of yours is missing.

A TAR.GZ WORKS AT THE SAME DOORS (.tgz and a plain .tar too): choose it
or drop it, and the game — which can only mount a zip — repacks it into
a zip of the same name, once, in the folder of its kind
(my_skin.tar.gz -> skins/my_skin.zip, cards.tar.gz -> cardpacks/
cards.zip; the Skin section shows "Repacking my_skin.tar.gz… 37%" while
it does, a few seconds for the card art), and from then on wears that
zip. Your tar is left where it was. Beside the game the same: put
skin/original_skin.tar.gz (or .tgz, .tar) or skin/cardart.tar.gz where
the zip would go, and the first start with no zip of that kind repacks
it into the game's own skins/ or cardpacks/ folder, from where every
later start reads it — the tar is read once and left alone.

THE SKIN FOLDER INSTEAD OF THE ZIP: if you would rather work with loose
files — `python3 import_original.py /path/to/1997/game` beside this file
fills original_skin/ from your own copy — tick "Use the skin folder
instead of the zip" in Options > Skin. From the next start the folder
alone dresses the game and the zip stays closed: a file missing from
the folder is drawn by the game, not taken from the zip. Unticked, the
zip is worn and a file in the folder still overrides the same file in
the zip.

CARD PACKS live in the card folder, cardpacks/: every zip there is
mounted when the game starts, in name order, the first to hold a
picture winning. A pack holds skin/cardart/<card_name_in_snake_case>.jpg
or .png. Choose one in Options > Skin (the Card folder row names the
folder and every pack in it) or drop it on the window; it is copied
into the folder under its own name (a tar.gz is repacked into a zip
there — see section 3). Loose pictures in a `cardart/`
subfolder of any of the places above are read too:

    <this folder>/skin/cardart/serra_angel.jpg      (loose)
    cardpacks/cardart.zip -> skin/cardart/serra_angel.jpg (a pack)

THE PLACES ARE YOURS TO MOVE. Options > Skin shows every one of them
by its full path, and each is a key in settings.cfg (the screen names
the file), under [options]:

    skin_zip           the skin zip worn — written for you when you
                       choose one; blank means the game's own pick
    skin_folder        the skin folder           (original_skin/)
    cardpacks_folder   the card folder           (cardpacks/)
    portraits_folder   your portraits            (portraits/)
    music_folder       your music                (music/)
    use_skin_folder    true: the folder instead of the zip (the tick box)

A value is an absolute path, or `user://...` for the folder above; a
leading `~` is your home. The game reads them when it starts; none is
written into the file until you change it, so a key you never touched
keeps following the game's default. For example:

    [options]
    music_folder="~/Music/shandalar"
    cardpacks_folder="/srv/mtg/packs"

DECKS the game shipped are read-only, and a deck of yours may not take
one of their names. That is the 1997 manual's own rule (p.148): "If you
load and change one of the creature decks used in the full game, you must
save your version of the deck under a new name." Saving over one turns
into Save As, with the new name already filled in.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CARD ART — THE ONE THING YOU COLLECT YOURSELF
---------------------------------------------------------------------

897 cards. The art is not in the 1997 install in any usable form, and
it is not shipped with the game either — the pictures are Scryfall's,
on a licence of their own, so what ships is the script that fetches
them, beside this file:

    python3 fetch_card_art.py --out cardart/
    python3 mtg_assets.py --from-cardart cardart/ --out cardart.zip

Needs Python 3 and a network connection, nothing else. It takes a few
minutes (it is deliberately polite to the API — about eight requests a
second), it SKIPS what it already has, so an interrupted run just carries
on where it stopped, and it prints what it could not fetch rather than
stopping. The second line zips the folder the way the game reads it —
a card pack: choose it in Options > Skin, drop it on the window, put it
in skin/ beside the game as skin/cardart.zip, or in the card folder
(section 3). The same two lines make a pack for a card set of the
future, too: the game wears every pack in the folder.

Without it, cards draw with a clean placeholder and are perfectly
playable — every name, cost, type, rules text and picture frame is there,
only the illustration is missing.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BUILDING THE ART ARCHIVE FROM YOUR OWN 1997 DISC
---------------------------------------------------------------------

If you own the original and would rather import it yourself than use
the original_skin.zip download:

    python3 mtg_assets.py                          explains what it needs
    python3 mtg_assets.py --check   /path/to/game  looks, reports, writes nothing
    python3 mtg_assets.py --install /path/to/game  imports, writes a zip

It reads your install and never writes to it. The zip it produces is a
skin zip — a `skin/` folder inside — so choose it in Options > Skin, drop
it onto the game's window, or put it where the download goes:

    cp shandalar-art.zip <this folder>/skin/original_skin.zip

To have the same material as loose files instead — the skin folder,
section 3, for the "Use the skin folder instead of the zip" tick box:

    python3 import_original.py --check   /path/to/game
    python3 import_original.py           /path/to/game

A partial install makes a partial archive and that is fine — every asset
is independent. To draw a skin of your own, SKIN.txt is the list of
everything the game wears, with each file's format, size and name.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TESTING DECKS — THE DECK LAB
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The same headless simulator the game's own AI was tuned with ships here.
It plays whole duels with no graphics and reports each win rate with the
interval that says how much of it to believe.

    ./deck_lab.sh --help
    ./deck_lab.sh --deck-a res://decks/big_green.deck \
                  --deck-b res://decks/blue_skies.deck --games 200
    ./deck_lab.sh --matrix res://decks/ --games 200

The 317 decks that shipped with the game live inside it and are addressed
as `res://decks/...`; a deck of your own is an ordinary path. Results go
to a folder you name with --out: a readable report.txt, a results.json, a
matchups.csv and two SVG charts.

It is the game binary doing the work — same engine, same rules, same AI
that just played your duel — so a number it gives you is a number about
the game you are actually playing. DECKLAB.md beside this file is the
full manual.

A word about how many games: 200 tells you very little. The tool says so
in words at the end of every run, and `--help` has the table.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE COIN TOSS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The duel opens on a tossed coin, and in 1997 that was a movie:
COINTOSS_Heads.AVI and COINTOSS_Tails.AVI, 61 frames each at 320x240.
They are Microsoft Video 1 (CRAM) — not the Indeo they were long taken
for — and neither Python nor Godot can play them, so they are converted
into sprite sheets when the art is imported.

Both halves ship in `skin/`: the finished sheets, and the two original
AVIs in `skin/movies/` beside them. Carrying the source matters, because
a conversion without its source is a dead end — if you installed the art
on a machine with no decoder and have since installed ffmpeg:

    python3 mtg_assets.py --transcode-movies skin/

rebuilds the sheets from the movies already there, with no 1997 disc
needed. Building your own archive keeps the movies too; --no-bundle-movies
leaves them out if you would rather save the 4.5 MB.

Without any of it the toss plays a coin the game draws itself.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Unreadable text on a panel, a missing picture, a card with no frame: the
game is almost certainly finding SOME of the art and not the rest. Run

    python3 mtg_assets.py --check /path/to/your/1997/install

which reports on eight groups of files separately, and re-import.

To go back to the drawn look entirely, rename `skin/` to `skin.off/`.
Nothing breaks; the game is tested that way.
